# The cooperative-economy atlas — and the build-first decision

**2026-07-02 corrective cut, completed (Fable 5 subagents, ultracode).**

This is the run [Fable 5's review](../2026-07-02-cognitive-economy-atlas/README.md#addendum--fable-5-review-2026-07-02-added-post-hoc)
prescribed after finding that the [adversarial-half atlas](../2026-07-02-cognitive-economy-atlas/README.md)
killed 28 domains with one correlated, leakage-defaulted judge and never
measured the substrate's *cooperative* half. It supersedes the quota-truncated
INTERIM. **93 agents, ~4.5M tokens, every
judgment-bearing agent on the Fable 5 model** — symmetric kill-verification,
decorrelated non-leakage generation and judges, the type-surface kernel's own
feasibility test, and a Fable-judged build-first decision. Raw:
`the run's full result artifact (private)`.

The verdict is not what the interim advocacy hoped — and that is the point of
doing it properly.

## 0. Result in one paragraph

**Fable's *mechanism* critique was right; her *magnitude* hope was wrong; the
original D1 call comes out stronger.** Symmetric verification of the 28 kills
(advocate → adversarial verifier, default `kill_stands`) collapsed the interim's
one-sided "7 resurrections" to **1 verified false-kill, 16 downgraded-partial
(only 3 load-bearing), 10 kills standing** — a corrected still-dead rate of
**~96%, harder than the original 88%, not softer.** The cooperative half, when
generated fresh (24 domains) and run through its *own* decorrelated non-leakage
gauntlet, produced **zero survivors**: every candidate was killed by a *named,
live 2026 incumbent that already ships credit/attribution/propagating
settlement* (Numerai Erasure, the MLC, thanks.dev + Socket, RSL Collective,
Story Protocol, Ocean datatokens, Substra/Owkin, Civitai, Braintrust…). The
organ the run was built to rescue turns out to be the **more crowded and
better-served of the two.** The Fable decision judge — which *grepped the repo*
and caught that the lone cooperative crossover isn't even in canon — ruled
**build D1 (third-party security research over sealed vendor artifacts) first**,
and the cooperative measurement makes that case *stronger* by eliminating where
a rival build could have come from. Separately, Fable's kernel-feasibility test
landed a fair hit on the [type-surface cut](../2026-07-02-premier-type-surface/README.md):
its Tier-1 "refinement/PCC" branding is **rebranding** — hash/subset checks
borrowing formal-methods prestige, "a vibe standing in for a mechanism."

## 1. Verified kill audit — symmetric skepticism, applied

The interim run had a resurrection *advocate* only; the quota died before
verification, so "7 false-kills" was an advocate's upper bound. This run added
the missing half: a Fable adversarial verifier attacks each resurrection, web-
checking whether the incumbent *already* provides the "missing" cooperative
layer and whether the cited gap is load-bearing. Default: `kill_stands`.

**Of 27 audited kills** (see §5 open-Q7 on the 27-vs-28 count gap):

| Verdict | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `verified_false_kill` | **1** | Space-traffic conjunction coordination — and even this is judge-doubted (§3) |
| `downgraded_partial` | 16 | the kill's incumbent-parity story was overstated, but **only 3 are load-bearing** (EU pay-equity, AI training-data long-tail, personal-data annotation unions); the other 13 are footnote-grade — still practically dead, just not for exactly the first-stated reason |
| `kill_stands` | 10 | resurrection failed outright; original kill correct |

**Corrected still-dead rate: 26/27 ≈ 96%** (counting only `verified_false_kill`
as a true reversal). The interim README hedged that the true false-kill rate was
"2/28 or 12/28"; the answer is **1/27 — the floor of that range, not the
middle.**

**What this means for Fable's critique.** The *mechanism* she named is real and
confirmed: all 3 load-bearing corrections share the identical structure — the
original kill correctly found a trusted transport/access-control incumbent (a
clean room, a bureau, a regulator) but wrongly treated that as proof an
attribution/settlement incumbent *also* existed. That is the prong-1 conflation
she diagnosed. But empirically it **did not launder into many wrong verdicts** —
the correlated-judge screen mostly got the calls right and merely narrated a few
with an overstated incumbent story. The methodology defect was real; its
object-level cost was ~1 clean miss out of 27. The self-demonstrating exhibit:
on the very first kill (antitrust benchmarking), the resurrection cited the
real Agri Stats / RealPage consent decrees — and the Fable verifier *attacked
its own side*: "the cited remedies fix leakage, not credit… the advocate
borrowed real damning enforcement history and applied it to the wrong layer" →
downgraded. That attack is exactly the step the interim run could not run.

## 2. Cooperative generation — zero survivors, and why

Six decorrelated non-leakage lenses (attribution-economy, personal-sovereignty,
worker-method, trust-bootstrapping, provenance-propagation, estimated-
heterogeneous) generated **24 distinct cooperative domains.** The non-leakage
gauntlet — a skeptic playing the CEO of the incumbent cooperative-accounting
rail, forbidden from killing merely on "parties trust each other" — **killed all
24.** Not near-misses: each died to a specific, named, live-2026 counterfactual.

The clusters and their gravediggers:

- **Quant-signal markets** → Numerai's Erasure protocol (live ERC-20
  staking/payout since 2019, public scoring oracle).
- **Music/media rights propagation** → the MLC (Music Modernization Act
  statutory body, $3.11B distributed through June 2025, *contractually barred
  from taking a cut*, and already running the exact "ESTIMATED lane that funds a
  real payout without becoming a cliff" via its unmatched-pool market-share
  distribution).
- **OSS/dependency funding** → thanks.dev (auto-split across the transitive
  graph) + Socket/Coana reachability (decisive-vs-incidental reuse) + Sigstore/
  SLSA; and 1997-vintage DOJ-blessed SEP pools (MPEG LA, Avanci) as the original
  `AttributionShare` instrument.
- **AI training-data licensing** → RSL Collective (1,500+ publishers), Cloudflare
  Pay-Per-Crawl → Pay-Per-Use (*pivoted the literal week of this run*, 1 Jul
  2026), ProRata, Perplexity Publishers Program.
- **Model/agent derivative marketplaces** → Story Protocol Royalty Module + PIL,
  Civitai Creator Program (live 25/75 split).
- **Personal-data/health/genomic co-ops** → Ocean datatokens, Streamr Data
  Unions, Vana DataDAOs, Mozilla Data Collective (100%-to-contributor, 5% flat).
- **Institutional coopetition** → Substra/Owkin (a *production* blockchain ledger
  across all ten MELLODDY pharma partners, no central authority — literally the
  un-ownable neutral ledger, already shipped).

**The common crossing:** every candidate assumed a missing "neutral ledger for
credit / attribution / propagating settlement" — and in every case a named live
system already occupies that role, as a statutory/nonprofit collective, a
deployed smart-contract rail, or an ordinary flat-fee platform nobody treats as
extractive. **The cooperative "non-extractive propagating royalty" niche is not
under-served; it is dense.** That is a verdict on 2026 market saturation, not on
the quality of `market.ts` / `iptrade.ts` — but it means the substrate's
cooperative differentiator has little daylight, whereas its adversarial
differentiator (composition-charged egress metering over sealed, mutually-
distrusting data) remains structurally unclaimed.

**Honest limit (§5 open-Q1):** because the gauntlet killed all 24, the
decorrelated *judge panel* only had the 1 imported crossover to score. So the
zero-survivor result is verified by **incumbent counterfactual** but not yet
cross-examined by the panel — the "zero" itself deserves one symmetric
resurrection pass, the same discipline just applied to the adversarial kills.

organ we set out to rescue is the weaker half.

## 4. The build-first decision (Fable-judged)

> **Build D1 — third-party security research over sealed vendor artifacts —
> first.** Metered-egress vuln-disclosure bounty (`EgressBudget` /
> `CompositionState` / `CapacityEstimate` at the adversarial maximum) +
> `Bounty` / `EvaluatorOracle` acceptance + regression-window-gated
> `CreditSettlement` / `SettlementPayoutAuthorization`, wedged into **one
> CRA-regulated PSIRT design partner.**

The decision chain (Fable, high effort, having grepped canon and web-verified both
sides' anchors): D1 is **canon-anchored** (`memo-1-security-research.md`, judged
68/100, "depends on no new frontier item beyond the incumbent's own"),
**demand-proven** by a live dated crisis (HackerOne's Internet Bug Bounty
paused/cut payouts Apr–May 2026 as AI volume drove valid-submission rate from
~15% to <5% — the exact "bounties fund the *find*, not the *fix*" gap D1's
mechanism closes), **wedge-tractable** via a single unilaterally-adopting buyer
racing the CRA clock, and its **one binding dependency is independently
confirmed `practical_now`** by the kernel-feasibility test (the semiring
accountant = `workbench/ip_trade_sim/leakage.py`, 179 working, tested lines).
The cooperative organ returned zero competitors; the one crossover is
judge-doubted 2-for-2 and absent from canon.

**First 90 days:** sign one CRA-exposed PSIRT (embedded-firmware / TLS-library
vendor) as the sealed-telemetry design partner; ship the accountant (ported from
`leakage.py`, wired to real vuln-repro/VEX output), a role-separated
(`adversarial_review`, never `self_interested`) `EvaluatorOracle` scoring against
a pinned rubric, and `SettlementPayoutAuthorization` + `CreditSettlement` with
regression-window holdback/clawback so payout lands only once the shipped fix
survives. Bill a platform fee plus a cut of settled payouts. Land first revenue
before the 11 Sep 2026 CRA clock.

**What kills D1 (carried forward, not papered over):** verification-as-extraction
is *bounded, not solved* — a motivated evaluator can reconstruct more sealed
source from a metered repro+score+payout trail than a linear `EgressBudget`
charges for (harm-nonlinear-in-bits and no-aux-knowledge remain open frontier);
the `CompositionKey` canonicalization the accountant needs for honest cross-run
metering is itself research-horizon (subject/queryFamily/audience-hash derivation
untyped, no standard to borrow); one incident where a nominally-compliant repro
leaks a de-anonymizing amount of source collapses the mechanism's credibility;
the independent `EstimatorAttestation` (never self-reported bits) does not yet
exist in the corpus; and "counterparty-compilable kernel" stays *asserted, not
shown* until a second independent implementation agrees bit-for-bit on golden
traces.

**Update — the counterparty-compilable claim is now SHOWN for the accountant
sub-engine.** [`chambers/conformance/`](../../../../chambers/conformance/README.md)
carries a normative language-independent spec (`SPEC.md`) of the semiring egress
accountant and two implementations — a Python reference and a separate-language
Rust crate sharing no code — that agree **bit-for-bit on 31 golden traces / 195
decisions**, cross-checked in both harness directions and shown to fail on a
single flipped bit. The exercise also resolved a latent split the kill list did
not name: two of the three accountant copies (`leakage.py`, `egress.py`) charge
in `float` and compute `log2` in the decision path, so they are *not*
counterparty-compilable; `intro_clearing` already charged in integer millibits.
The resolution, now normative: **the counterparty-compilable boundary is the
estimation/accounting boundary** — `log2`/byte-ceilings belong to the attested,
replaceable estimator (which emits integer millibits), and the ledger core is
exact integer arithmetic. Residual debt, kept honest: the corpus is finite (not
a proof over all inputs), and the two implementations shared an author (a
genuinely separate implementer is still the stronger form — the delegation was
built and staged but the worker was reaped by the environment before it
produced a crate).

**Runnable slice:** [`workbench/d1_bounty/`](../../../d1_bounty/README.md)
now embodies this decision end-to-end — five deterministic lanes (honest
zero-touch payout · regression clawback · extraction campaign refused with a
demand-latched incident · oracle capture to human fallback · self-interested
estimator refused pre-oracle), hash-chained court files, tested. Two of the
kill-list items are *mechanized* there rather than papered over: the
`EstimatorAttestation` admissibility check exists and refuses self-metered
bits, and the incident semantics distinguish leakage (what crossed) from
demand (what was asked), so refusals do not launder an extraction campaign.
The counterparty-compilable caveat is carried verbatim in every lane's receipt
as an explicit non-claim: one process, one trusted object — a second
independent implementation agreeing bit-for-bit on golden traces is still owed.

## 5. Kernel-feasibility test — the type-surface cut's own medicine

Fable ran the substrate's `researchHorizonPlansMayNotGateLiveSettlement`
discipline on the [type-surface cut](../2026-07-02-premier-type-surface/README.md)'s
Tier-1 kernel. **Verdict: mixed, unevenly across the three sub-engines** — and it
resolves that cut's own open memo (§8.8/§11.3):

- **Graded (Bits × LeakageClass) semiring accountant → `practical_now`, not
  asserted but *demonstrated*.** `workbench/ip_trade_sim/leakage.py` (179 LOC,
  tested) already implements key-indexed cumulative bits, monotone addition, and
  ceiling-crossing refusal. This is the one sub-engine D1 needs — so D1's core
  technical risk is low. Caveat: it is one trusted shared object in one process;
  it has *not* demonstrated two independently-written adversarial implementations
  agreeing bit-for-bit over a signed ledger — the actual "counterparty-
  compilable" property.
- **MPST-style order monitor → buildable small-kLOC, but gated on lane-shape
  governance** (who authors/amends the committed global type). Institutional, not
  engineering — "no amount of kLOC reduces it." D1 doesn't need it (a single
  query-response repro isn't a multi-step session protocol).
- **"Refinement / PCC receipts" → rename, don't rebrand.** The concrete Tier-1
  items are hash-equality and set-subset relational validation over declared
  facts — "*not* proof-carrying code or refinement typing in the load-bearing
  sense; calling it 'refinement/PCC' borrows a research tradition's prestige for
  what is actually relational validation… the same move `runtime.ts`'s opening
  line forbids for 'secure execution' — a vibe standing in for a mechanism."

**This is a fair, sharp hit and it is now folded back into the type-surface cut's
open items.** Proposed law, mirroring `VerificationVerdict`'s partition at the
kernel-architecture level: `kernelFeasibilityIsTaggedPerSubEngineNeverBundled`,
so "the kernel does all three" can't silently promote a research-horizon
governance gap into a shipped-2026 guarantee.

## 6. Primitive pressure — reverse

Zero cooperative survivors ⇒ zero *forward* primitive pressure (no survivor to
type onto canon). Instead the 23 kills exert **reverse pressure**: each names the
*same already-shipped* `market.ts`/`iptrade.ts` primitives as "the thing this
domain wanted," and finds an incumbent already running a version — evidence the
existing cooperative primitives target a crowded niche, not evidence new records
are needed. The most damaging single hit: the **MLC's estimated black-box-pool
distribution and Numerai's non-extractive Erasure staking are live real-world
precedents for the canon's two most distinctive cooperative claims** (the
`ESTIMATED` lane; non-extractive `CreditSettlement`). CANON.md should arguably
reframe these as "validated against an existing pattern," not "a novel unlock."
**Untested, not vindicated:** `matching.ts` (purpose-blind mediated relations —
the canon's own personal-sovereignty origin, and half of Fable's "structurally
absent" gap) drew *zero* recurrence; no generated candidate was matching-shaped,
so that half of the cooperative organ remains as untested after this run as
before it.

## 7. Open questions worth their own memos

Several are self-critical by design — the run auditing its own honesty:

1. **The zero-survivor cooperative result needs the panel pass.** The judges only
   scored the 1 crossover; "0 of 24" is verified by counterfactual, not
   cross-examined by the decorrelated panel — it deserves the same symmetric
   resurrection just applied to the adversarial kills.
2. **D1's 68/100 needs a symmetric re-score.** It came from the same single-judge
   apparatus this run's doctrine flags as suspect for *kills*. Re-score the
   *survivor*, not just the kills, before treating 68 as load-bearing rather than
   provisional.
3. **Is `downgraded_partial` + `loadBearing:false` operationally distinct from
   `kill_stands`,** or is a soft label absorbing true reversals? The field doing
   the most synthesis work here has no published rubric for "load-bearing."
4. **`matching.ts` / personal-sovereignty was never generated.** "Cooperative
   organ measured, found weaker" holds for the attribution/royalty slice only;
   the canon's own origin half is untested — a targeted generator pass is owed
   before the conclusion generalizes.
5. **Should the sole `verified_false_kill` (space-traffic) get a typed canon
   proposal** purely to exercise the five-prong admission test against a real
   SDA/COMSPOC-shaped `SettlementConsortium`, even though nobody would build it?
6. **Reframe the MLC / Numerai precedents in CANON.md** — from "novel unlock" to
   "validated against an existing pattern," and decide whether that changes which
   cooperative pitch (if any) is worth generating a tenth time.
7. **Reconcile 27 vs 28.** One original adversarial kill is missing from this
   audit — track down which, and whether it's a transcription gap or a deliberate
   exclusion, before citing "27 of 28" downstream.

## 8. What this changes

- **The adversarial atlas's headline stands, harder.** The 88% kill rate does not
  fall; the verified number is ~96% still-dead. **D1 remains the build-first
  bet — now with the cooperative alternative measured and eliminated, not merely
  assumed weaker.**
- **Fable's review is vindicated as *method* and corrected as *magnitude*.** The
  correlated-judge defect was real (prong-1 conflation, 3 load-bearing cases);
  its practical cost was ~1 clean miss. Measuring the cooperative half was the
  right call *and* it returned a null result — which is itself a finding.
- **The type-surface cut owes a de-branding.** "Refinement/PCC" → "relational
  validator"; tag kernel feasibility per sub-engine; the semiring accountant is
  the piece to ship first, and `leakage.py` already proves it.

**Canon posture unchanged:** nothing here amends `primitives/CANON.md`. Zero new
primitives were admitted (no survivors to type). The proposed changes —
`kernelFeasibilityIsTaggedPerSubEngineNeverBundled`, the MLC/Numerai reframing,
the de-branding — are explicit proposals, each owing the five-prong gate.
