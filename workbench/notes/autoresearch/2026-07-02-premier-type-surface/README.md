# The premier type surface — 2026-07-02 autoresearch cut

**Question.** Which type system should Scry Chambers adopt as the premier
surface for modeling private work, staged disclosure, and priced crossings
between sovereign owners — and what does the current canon deliver cleanly
versus merely gesture at?

**Method.** 34 agents, ~2.9M tokens, raw result archived as
`the run's full result artifact (private)`. Three grounding readers (full
canon map, prior-cut digest, canonical crossing reconstruction) → seven
independent candidate advocates, each walking the same IP-trade crossing end to
end in its own nouns → four property audits (61 findings), every finding
adversarially re-verified against the modules (52 confirmed, 9 corrected,
0 refuted) → three contested-decision debates, each with pro/con advocates and
a code judge who checked every claim against the actual `.ts` before it counted
→ three non-expert owner personas → three balanced judges → one synthesis.
Every headline code claim below was additionally re-verified by hand against
the modules.

**Canon subordination.** `../../primitives/CANON.md` remains the source of
truth. This document argues; the modules decide. Every proposed change to canon
is listed explicitly in §10 — nothing here amends canon by existing.

## 0. The result in one paragraph

**Keep the status-quo vocabulary; ship the missing enforcement organ.** The
recommended surface is the **Checked Court File**: the branded-ADT canon
(core/entropy/environment/runtime/attention/market/matching/pricing/negotiation/iptrade)
stays the organizing ontology, and the sprint's center of gravity moves to a
counterparty-compilable sidecar kernel over the JSONL court file whose spec is
the union of the three formal candidates' checkable cores — MPST projection as
the ordering/session monitor, a graded `(Bits × LeakageClass)` semiring as the
composition arithmetic, and refinement-shaped obligations emitted as
proof-carrying receipt entries a counterparty re-checks with a kernel it
compiled itself. The binding constraint is sovereignty: a guarantee enforced by
*my* typechecker evaporates against a counterparty who does not run my
compiler. What survives the boundary is shared validatable vocabulary, hash
commitments, each party's own accountant, each party's own human gates — plus
exactly one thing the status quo lacks: **checkable derivation artifacts**
re-verified by the counterparty's own small kernel. That is refinement/PCC's
unique contribution, and the only formal artifact all three judges agreed
crosses the boundary.

### Why the status quo survives contact

Two structural facts, both verified against code, decide the shape of the
answer:

1. **Sovereignty filters guarantees.** Every candidate brief conceded that its
   static guarantee is per-compiler, hence per-sovereign. `negotiation.ts`'s
   premise — `neitherPartyOwnsTheLane`, `eachBoundaryIsGatedByItsOwnReviewStack`
   — makes this the design's own axiom. Only re-checkable artifacts cross.
2. **Failure classes are not equal.** The verified audits show the status
   quo's failures are *lies of omission* (unforced debits, unexecuted laws, one
   typed overclaim). Each rival adopted whole imports a worse class: certified
   wrongness (refinement statement bugs), the ε-halo (DP), fidelity-of-a-fiction
   via out-of-band side letters (MPST rigidity), declassify-to-compile
   laundering (DLM), c-list/court-file drift (ocap). The what-breaks judge
   rated omission the least toxic class in the set.

## 2. Adoption end to end

Each candidate walked the same crossing: Lab A sells Lab B a verified result
claim — `EscrowedClaim` committed by salted hash, doubly-sealed verifier
admitted by Grants from both parties with a verdict-only sink,
`VerificationVerdict` partitioned proven/trusted/unprovable, `PriceCross`
cleared from jointly-committed draws, `Settlement` gated on a non-empty verdict
tuple with `deliveredBinding == verifiedBinding`, per-party
`CrossChamberReceipt` with asymmetric caveats, everything ledgered and metered.

Where each stays low-friction and where it burdens owners:

- **Status quo** — friction is concentrated at exactly the right places (human
  `ownerDecision` per content disclosure, review cards not payloads) but the
  owner cannot tell which laws are *checked* versus *recited*; the invisible
  enforcement gradient is its worst legibility defect.
- **Refinement** — invisible to owners when proofs pass; brutal when a schema
  migration breaks 40 obligations at once. Counsel-legible ("here is the
  artifact your own kernel verified") if and only if the axiom manifest is
  printed beside the theorem.
- **MPST** — natural for the negotiation lane (the ladder *is* a session);
  hostile to the unanticipated human move ("can we add one more verification
  round?" = new lane shape). Needs a versioned, both-party-committed amendment
  path or parties route around it — and out-of-band side letters are
  undetectable by construction.
- **Ocap** — the steward's favorite (revocation that provably kills a running
  mount is the single most-wanted mechanism in the persona reports), invisible
  to counterparties.
- **Graded/DP/DLM** — all three are engine-room candidates; every persona
  scored their owner surfaces lowest. They must never be the vocabulary an
  owner sees, only the machinery under a `PlainAccount` sentence.

## 3. Property coverage — what is delivered vs. gestured

Four audits, 61 findings, each classified
`delivered_in_types / delivered_runtime_only / gestured / absent` and then
adversarially re-verified: **52 confirmed, 9 corrected, 0 refuted.** The
pattern: the record layer is genuinely strong; the *coupling* layer — the
places where a law says "X debits/blocks/binds" — is where delivery thins out.

### 3a. How little a cleared crossing reveals

- **Delivered:** commitments-not-curves (`PriceDistribution` crosses only
  `commitmentHash` + family + `supportBucket`); sampler draw visibility typed
  `Extract<Visibility, "system_secret" | "owner_private">`; both outcomes of a
  `PriceCross` carry mandatory `compositionKeyId + egressDebitId +
  ledgerEntryId`.
- **CONFIRMED defect (the cut's headline):** `PriceCross.clearedPrice?:
  CreditMicros` is exact and unbucketed on the same record that asserts
  `counterpartyLearns: "one_bit"`. Under `ClearingRule "midpoint"`, exact
  clearedPrice plus one's own private draw determines the counterparty's draw
  *exactly* — a fresh exact draw from each committed curve leaks per cleared
  trade. The substrate's own precision discipline (`exactCountsAllowed: false`,
  `scoreBucket`) is violated by its own clearing record. The `"one_bit"` claim
  is true only on the `no_cross` branch.
- **Gestured:** `EscrowedClaim` commit topology (how many claims, which
  classes) is unmetered; `VerificationVerdict`'s prose lists are
  capacity-unmetered; `CrossChamberReceipt` carries no `noPerfectSecrecyClaim`
  and no caveat requirement — the one receipt an *outside* counterparty holds
  is the one receipt not forced to state limits.
- **Absent:** `BindingCommitment.quantizationClass: string` is the only raw
  free string on the crossing surface — a covert channel.

### 3b. How repeated queries compose against a budget

- **Delivered:** the vocabulary is the strongest part of the substrate —
  `CompositionKey` (6 components), `CompositionState` with monotone
  `cumulativeStructuredBits`, adversarial-maximum `CapacityEstimate` split by
  channel, `EstimatorAttestation` naming every estimator, the
  numeric-AND-ordinal conjunction gate.
- **CONFIRMED:** the conjunction is a *doc comment* — `{releaseGate: "allow",
  numericAccountantTripped: true}` is a representable contradiction; nothing
  relates the fields. `CompositionState` does not reference the `EgressDebit`s
  it sums, so the authoritative Σ is unauditable from its own record and
  rollback is representable. Which `CapacityEstimate` fields sum into the Σ
  (`metadataBits`? `sideChannelBits`?) is specified nowhere. Composition is
  **computed nowhere in canon** — `ObservableEvent` does not force a debit;
  matching's bucket emissions and `CreditSettlement` feed no accountant at all.
- **CONFIRMED (elegant leak):** `CreditSettlement.amount` is an exact,
  egress-uncoupled channel that inverts through a *published* `PriceSchedule`
  (a monotone step function of `oracleScore`) to the very score band
  `MATCHING_LAWS` forbids releasing.
- **Load-bearing unknown:** `CompositionKey` canonicalization
  (`subjectHash`/`queryFamilyHash` derivation, who hashes) is untyped; every
  composition argument in both debates silently depends on it.

### 3c. Incidental signals — timing, absence, refusal

- **Delivered:** `ObservableKind` includes `"absence"`, `"timing"`,
  `"notification_timing"`; `ConsentPolicy.denialVisibleToCounterparty: false`
  with `timeoutDecision`; outward timestamps declared emissions (core.ts doc);
  `walkAwayTimingIsItselfAnEmission` named as law.
- **Gestured/absent (CONFIRMED):** these are the substrate's most honest law
  texts with the least type support. Run-less refusals have no typed trigger
  binding them to an `absence` event; denial-timing indistinguishability has no
  mechanism (nothing couples the consent path to a hold-until-deadline
  `ObfuscationPlan`); walk-away/freeze has no carrier — `NegotiationLane.state
  "frozen"/"abandoned"` transitions carry no `ledgerEntryId`, no
  `LeakageEstimate`; there is **no bucketed timestamp type anywhere** — every
  outward-crossing time field (`RevealStage.openedAt`, `ConsentRecord.decidedAt`,
  `NotificationAttempt.sentAt`, `CoinCommitment.revealedAt`,
  `Release.releasedAt`) is a raw RFC3339 `Timestamp` despite
  `outwardTimestampsAreEmissions`; `NotificationAttempt` carries a
  `LeakageEstimate` but never debits a budget; budget-exhaustion blocks are
  themselves uncharged signals.

### 3d. Whether an honest receipt states its own limits

- **Delivered:** the strongest property. `ReceiptPayload.noPerfectSecrecyClaim:
  true` and `RunClaim.support` non-empty tuples make the flagship sentences
  true *by construction*; `RunClaimPredicate` is a closed union with
  `not_a_privacy_proof` first-class; `tee_quote` support is `caveated: true` by
  type; `VerificationVerdict`'s only success state is `verified_partitioned`.
- **Gestured (CONFIRMED):** support *relevance* is unchecked — a `RunClaim`
  citing a `reject` review verdict as support for `release_review_passed`
  typechecks. Caveat *presence* is forced but *content* is free
  `MinimizedText`; iptrade's four standing non-claims have no
  `ReceiptCaveatCode`s. `EnvironmentReceiptPayload.claimClass: "tee_quote"` is
  assertable with no quote hash, no trust root, no observer — evidence-free.
  Nothing states `ReceiptClaim ⊆ releasedFields` — not even a law names it.
- **Partition laundering:** `CrossChamberReceipt.verifiedClaimIds` and
  `EscrowedClaim.state: "verified"` both flatten proven/trusted/unprovable back
  into a boolean — violating iptrade's cardinal rule at the negotiation
  boundary itself.

## 4. Contested decisions — code-judged

Each debate: two advocates argued as hard as honesty allows; a code judge
re-read the modules and ruled per claim (`confirmed / refuted /
not-decidable-from-code`).

### 4a. Sampling vs. exact overlap → **keep sampling; the cleared branch is wrong as typed**

Neither advocate's strong form survived the code. Pro's
structural-incompatibility claims were refuted (the `mpc_2pc` bar is scoped to
`VerificationPlan`, not clearing; `"one_bit"` is asserted, not derived; and the
audit-privacy asymmetry is false — auditing a sample against `commitmentHash`
reveals *"the full parameterization plus salt"* to the auditor). Con's
replacement also failed (a point-family "noise opt-out" is wrong; deterministic
re-query is not free — every cross debits per event). Sampling's real,
code-compatible virtue, which the con undersold: under per-event bit metering,
noisy probes yield the attacker only ~log(N) bits of curve information per N
charged bits, versus a full bit per charged bit for noiseless bisection —
sampling makes the tripwire accountant conservative instead of exactly-spent.
Whether a `practical_small` 2PC comparison should replace the draw-seeing
sampler TTP is genuinely open: the modules permit it; only prose rejected it;
per CANON the modules decide.

### 4b. Commit-then-reveal staging → **right spine; three confirmed soundness gaps**

The alternative (consortium-escrowed conditional reveal) fails on two code
facts: the doubly-sealed verifier is typed as a *verdict-only sink*, and a
release-capable escrow holding day-one plaintext of all claims — including for
lanes that abort at stage 1 — is a strictly broader TTP; and
`contentDisclosureRequiresAHumanOwnerDecision` forbids the standing delegation
that "one deposit plus adjudicated atomic release" requires. But the current
implementation under-delivers its own discipline: (i) **no
`revealMatchesCommitment` law** — nothing binds the revealed artifact to
`commitmentHash` (iptrade closed the exact analogous gap with
`deliveredBindingMustEqualVerifiedBinding`); an unchecked opening makes the
commitment ceremonial; (ii) **the stage gate runs on a boolean-verified
backdoor** — `RevealStage.requiredClaimIds` key `EscrowedClaim.state
"verified"`, never the verdict partition; (iii) **`"withdrawn"` permits
selective-withdrawal cherry-picking** — no committed-set attestation, no
withdrawal-is-an-emission law. Fix, don't relitigate. Frontier #2 (worker-side
fair exchange) gets the same discipline extended to `market.ts`, exactly as
CANON already prescribes.

### 4c. Bucket quantization → **right decision; five confirmed implementation defects**

Pro wins the central soundness argument: one emission from a closed 6-symbol
`Bucket` alphabet carries ≤ log2(6) bits *regardless of adversarial boundary
placement*, so summed per-emission capacity is a true upper bound wherever
debits actually accrue — and noise would require exactly the
auxiliary-knowledge model frontier #5 admits the substrate lacks. But as typed:
(a) the score→bucket boundary map is committed nowhere, while pricing one file
away commits `commitmentHash` and `rubricHash` — an indefensible asymmetry;
(b) the `"one_bit"` cleared-branch overclaim (§3a); (c) the
`CreditSettlement.amount` score-band inversion (§3b); (d) `PriceFamily
"point"` makes draws deterministic, converting each cross into a reliable
threshold probe with no distinguished debit; (e) matching's bucket emissions
are not type-forced into `EgressDebit` the way `PriceCross` is. Whether
randomized quantization ever becomes obligatory is genuinely open but strictly
downstream of frontier #5.

## 5. The recommendation — the Checked Court File, in tiers

### Tier 0 — canon record/law fixes (ship first; a substrate carrying its own `"one_bit"` lie cannot host anyone else's theorems)

All from confirmed/corrected findings and debate rulings; every item is a
proposed canon change (§10), enforceable by `tsc` the day it lands:

- **pricing.ts** — make `counterpartyLearns` outcome-dependent (`"one_bit"`
  only on `no_cross`; on `cleared`, either bucket `clearedPrice` / clear at a
  committed schedule point, or type the field honestly as
  `"cleared_price_and_own_draw_inversion"` and charge the debit at price-space
  capacity). Bar or surcharge `PriceFamily "point"`.
- **iptrade.ts** — `quantizationClass` → closed enum. Add `trustRootId`
  (and `observerId`) to `runtime.ts` `tee_quote` support — it already carries
  `quoteHash: Hash; caveated: true` (`runtime.ts:29`), so `quoteHash` is *not*
  the gap there. The evidence-free record is `environment.ts`
  `EnvironmentReceiptPayload`, which needs all three
  (`quoteHash`/`trustRootId`/`observerId`). [Corrected per Fable 5 review —
  see §11.]
- **negotiation.ts** — rekey `RevealStage.requiredClaimIds` to
  `Id<"VerificationVerdict">[]` (kills the boolean-verified backdoor); add
  `revealMatchesCommitment` and `withdrawalIsAnEmission`/committed-set laws;
  freeze/abandon and `EscrowedClaim` transitions gain `ledgerEntryId` +
  `LeakageEstimate`; `perPartyEgressBudgetIds` → non-empty per-party tuple;
  `CrossChamberReceipt` carries `verdictIds` + required
  `noPerfectSecrecyClaim: true`.
- **core.ts** — `ReceiptCaveatCode` + 5 codes (the four standing non-claims:
  `extraction_leak_possible`, `verifier_saw_both_secrets`,
  `receipt_not_a_contract`, `reputation_root_wrong_for_one_shot`; plus
  `facts_declared_not_verified`); new law
  `receiptClaimsAreASubsetOfReleasedFields`; a `BucketedTimestamp` carrier
  (`Timestamp × Precision`) on every outward-crossing time field.
- **entropy.ts** — `CompositionState` gains `ledgerEntryId` + the
  `egressDebitIds` it sums + an `EntropyReview` back-reference (rollback
  unrepresentable, Σ auditable from its own record); a law fixing structured-sum
  membership; a `CompositionKey` canonicalization annex with a named hasher
  principal.
- **matching/market/attention** — mandatory `compositionKeyId + egressDebitId`
  on `CandidateRelation` release transitions, on released `CreditSettlement`,
  and on `NotificationAttempt`; new **`QuantizerCommitment`** record (salted
  hash of boundary positions + input domain, mirroring
  `commitmentHash`/`rubricHash`) referenced by every released bucket.
- **Cross-cutting** — badge every law const and owner surface with its
  enforcement tier: **[T]** type / **[L]** unexecuted law / **[A]** runtime
  accountant / **[H]** human. The invisible gradient is the status quo's worst
  confirmed legibility defect.

### Tier 1 — the checker organ

`check_court_file.py` grown into a re-checkable kernel (target: small enough
for a counterparty to compile and audit), conformance-tested against
`primitives/`. Three sub-engines over the JSONL ledger:

1. **Session monitor** (from MPST): a hash-committed global type per *lane
   shape* (`laneShapeHash`, committed at lane open exactly like `rubricHash` —
   a shape change is a new shape, with a versioned both-party-committed
   amendment path). Projections validate ledger event order — the eight
   ordering laws become checked — and emit role-localized blame-at-message-*k*
   as a ledgered incident: court-file-native breach evidence.
2. **Semiring accountant** (from graded/QTT): Key-indexed
   `(Bits × LeakageClass)` with pointwise addition and product-order
   release-gate subsumption — the conjunction gate becomes a computed check.
   `enumValueBits`/`orderingBits`/`fieldPresenceBits` *derived* from schema
   (log2 of legal choice space), closing the self-interested-estimator hole for
   structured channels; `sideChannelBits`/`textBitsUpperBound` remain attested
   axioms printed as separate receipt lines — derived and declared never fused.
   The sim's `leakage.py` is the empirically-validated reference
   implementation, upgraded from its 2-tuple key to the canonical 6-component
   `CompositionKey`.
3. **Refinement checks** (from PCC): `deliveredBinding == verifiedBinding` over
   *both* hashes; `revealMatchesCommitment`;
   `releaseFieldsAreAReviewedSubsetOfTheSink`; `receiptClaims ⊆
   releasedFields`; retention-outlives-claims arithmetic; ledger-height
   ordering witnesses; a predicate→evidence-kind/polarity map for `RunClaim`
   support relevance; typed triggers binding run-less refusals,
   budget-exhaustion blocks, and denial timing to `"absence"`/`"timing"`
   events.

New `RunClaimSupport` kinds (closed-union extensions): `graded_derivation`
(semiring derivation hash, re-checkable), `authority_topology` (c-list hash),
`session_conformance` (projection transcript hash).
`PlainAccount.whatDidNotCross`/`whatItCannotPromise` become *compiled* from
budget residuals + axiom manifests + missing witnesses, with a
counterexample→plain-sentence translation layer so kernel failures are
owner-legible. A fact-canonicalization standard (serialization + hashing of
court-file records) is pinned by hash at lane open alongside `laneShapeHash`
and kernel version.

### Tier 2 — vocabulary and mechanism grafts

- **From ocap:** run the doubly-sealed verifier, sampler, and settlement escrow
  as confined vats (SES/Endo or Cap'n Proto) whose complete c-list is
  enumerable and ships as `authority_topology` support — *"no network reference
  existed to invoke"* replaces *"we configured it off"*. Membrane revocation
  makes `Grant.revokedAt` an *effect* that kills mounts/tools/model access
  mid-`Run`, shipped with a "what dies if I cut this" owner preview, a capDL
  court-file projection, and conformance tests — or the graft creates the drift
  break it closes. Receipts state the guarantee dies at the owner's own monitor
  edge.
- **From DLM:** principal-indexed reader sets generalizing `Visibility` for
  ≥3-party lanes and per-member consortium visibility; conjunctive ownership
  (`owner: A∧B`) as the representational core of frontier #8. Vocabulary only —
  no flow checker, no acts-for hierarchy. Proposed, shelved until a ≥3-party
  lane actually ships.
- **From DP:** exactly one mechanism — SVT-typed threshold verdicts over the
  buyer's private eval, admissible *solely* as a `proven[]` partition entry
  naming adjacency relation, mechanism hash, and a new `TrustRoot` kind
  `"dp_typed_runtime"` (`practical_now`, degrades to `explicit_unprovable`).
  Never a chamber-level property. ε and `Bits` branded as non-summable
  currencies preemptively.

### Tier 3 — explicitly shelved

Full DLM checking (a TS embedding collapses into the existing brands),
compile-time linearity (monitors are the deployable form), a general DP
language, and any Lean/LH rewrite of the data plane (the archived Haskell
kernel remains the migration path if the sidecar kernel later warrants a
proof-checked core). Refusals recorded so absence is a decision, not drift.

**The admission cage for every graft:** every imported guarantee lands as a
partition entry (`proven[]/trusted[]/unprovable[]`) or a caveated
`RunClaimSupport` kind — never as a chamber-level property. The receipt
discipline is what stands between each graft and its own halo.

## 7. Tradeoffs accepted

1. **An interval of unexecuted prose.** Until the kernel ships, every Tier-0
   law addition is more [L]-tier text. The plan front-loads what `tsc` enforces
   and funds the kernel first; the honest statement is that this makes the law
   tier *checkable*, not that it is checked today.
2. **Bounded imports of worse failure classes.** Statement bugs (mitigated by
   scoping theorems to audit-confirmed gaps and giving statement review the
   CANON admission ceremony — not eliminated); lane-shape rigidity (amendment
   path helps; out-of-band side letters remain undetectable by construction);
   c-list drift (capDL projection + conformance tests funded with the graft, or
   the graft is refused); the ε/proof halos (caged culturally by the partition
   rule, not typed).
