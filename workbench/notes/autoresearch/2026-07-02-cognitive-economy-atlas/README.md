# The cognitive-economy atlas — and a doctrine for differentiated deep research

**2026-07-02 autoresearch cut.**

Two questions, answered in one pass because they are the same question. *How do
we do more differentiated deep research?* and *which critical economies does this
substrate uniquely unlock?* — because the method that finds real domains is the
method that refuses fake ones, and a run's value is measured by what its own
machinery kills.

**Method.** 55 agents, ~2.7M tokens (raw:
`the run's full result artifact (private)`). Nine **decorrelated** domain
generators (regulatory-bind, coopetition, dead-market autopsy, 2026-tech-unlock,
incumbent-inadequacy, public-goods, agent-native, mechanism-gap,
catastrophe-demand) produced 65 candidates → deduped to 32 → each run through an
**adversarial counterfactual gauntlet** (a skeptic playing the CTO of the
strongest incumbent — clean rooms, deployed MPC, consortium bureaus, TTP+NDA —
with `killed=true` as the default, external anchors web-verified) → **28 killed,
4 survived** (~88% kill rate) → survivors typed onto the canon with
admission-test-screened missing primitives → three judges → three deep memos.
Run in parallel: a **forensic pass** over this repo's own prior autoresearch
archives, asking which orchestration moves actually produced confirmed findings.
One memo (dual-use hazard screening) and one judge (wedge-tractability) came back
degenerate; both are noted and routed around, not hidden.

## Part I — The doctrine (the answer to "how")

The forensic pass found a **refutation gradient** across four prior runs. Read
it as a *direction*, not a controlled experiment — framing is confounded with
subject (the ip-trades pass killed 84% partly because IP-trade feasibility
genuinely is ~84% infeasible in 2026, not purely because refutation-framing has
5× the killing power). The percentages are illustrative; the ordering is the
claim. [Softened per Fable 5 review — see the addendum.]

| Run | Orchestration move | Kill / correction rate |
|---|---|---|
| ip-trades feasibility pass | optimistic generation → hardnosed-cryptographer refutation of every cell | **84% of cells corrected** (only 7 of 46 held) |
| type-surface debates | pro/con advocates → **code judge** ruling per claim | 16% of claims refuted; 13/61 `not-decidable-from-code` |
| type-surface property audits | findings generated *already grounded in modules* → adversarial verify | 0% refuted, 15% corrected |
| premier-use-cases walkthrough | advocacy/demonstration, **no adversarial layer** | unmeasurable — and shipped ≥2 claims (`oracleAuthorMayNotBeWorker: true`; "a failed cross tells each side exactly one bit") that later refutation-framed runs **proved false within 48h** |

The gradient *is* the doctrine: refutation-framed passes kill ~84%; code-judges
over advocacy kill ~16%; verification over artifact-grounded generation corrects
without refuting; **advocacy with no verifier kills nothing and ships lies.**
This atlas run reproduced the top of the gradient at the domain layer: 88%
killed.

Four standing rules follow.

**1. Decorrelate the generators — and the judges.** Nine orthogonal generating
*functions*, not nine samples of one prompt: sameness averages into mush. Every
survivor carried 2–5 lenses; the two strongest carried four-plus — cross-lens
convergence is signal. The sharper lesson: **decorrelate the judges and publish
where they diverge, don't blend them.** Here the economics judge and the
what-breaks judge agreed on the #1 domain but explicitly disagreed on the
bottom two (genomic vs. peer review) — that named disagreement axis is more
useful than a smooth composite rank. Fund one judge per orthogonal failure mode
(does it pay? / what breaks? / is the mechanism only-here?), plus a
shared-blind-spot critic above the lens layer (disciplinary decorrelation does
not decorrelate *worldview* — the 207-agent stress test found all eight lenses
shared owner-centrism).

**2. Verify with the load-bearing test.** Cite-or-die applied to markets: is the
substrate's delta *load-bearing for the domain's economics, or decorative?*
Every one of the 28 kills names a specific deployed incumbent (Decentriq, AWS
Clean Rooms DP, Catena-X CX-0123, Salv Bridge, COSMIC, CrowdStrike OverWatch,
YCharOS, Slingshot Beacon, CMS HFPP, SWIFT Compliance Analytics) and shows the
delta is already shipped, decorative to the governing legal test, or actively
harmful. Refuse any survivor whose differentiator is *asserted* rather than
*shown* to be the thing an incumbent structurally cannot do.

**3. Prong-or-decline.** Pre-screen every proposed canon change against the five
admission prongs (Boundary / Lifecycle / Composition / Owner / Receipt).
Survivors were *forced to decline* primitives that fail — `OwnerQuorum` and
`DualControlEscalationAuthority` failed Composition and were routed to the open
frontier rather than asserted; `PanelCountersignature` passes only as a
pointer+quorum wrapper, not an emission-bearing record. This is the code-judge's
`not-decidable-from-code` slot moved to design time: it converts fake closure
into honest open frontier.

**4. The kill-list is the deliverable — so verify the kills.** A run that ships
survivors without a kill-list, or primitives without a decline-list, has not
verified — it has advocated. Advocacy and walkthrough prose are *generator fuel
only*; the terminal artifact is the refutation residue. **But a raw kill-count
is a corrupt metric** (Fable 5 review): rewarding kills with no counterweight
for *false* kills produces a screen that celebrates its own bias. This run
committed exactly that error (see addendum): the 28 kills got a **single,
correlated, kill-defaulted judge** while the 4 survivors got three — the
deliverable received the *weakest* judging in the run. The rule, corrected: the
kill-list must be **decorrelated-judged and adversarially resurrected** to the
same standard as the survivor findings (the survivor property audit corrected
9 of 61; the kill-list was corrected 0 of 28 because nobody attacked it). The
count that predicts value is not kills but **kills that survive a resurrection
pass** plus survivors that survive a symmetric one.

## Part II — The atlas (the answer to "which economies")

### The seven-part kill screen (from 28 autopsies)

Most kills fired on 3–5 of these at once; each was described as independently
sufficient. This is the reusable screen — apply it before building anything.

1. **A trusted TTP already exists / holds something worse.** Sovereignty is not
   load-bearing when a regulator can mandate participation or a neutral body
   already holds the more sensitive asset. *Fired on:* biothreat screening
   (IBBIS holds the hazard DB), epidemic warning (WHO Hub), cyber-insurance
   (Lloyd's holds every syndicate return), media forensics (World Press Photo),
   claims reconciliation (CMS HFPP has pooled rival payers since 2012),
   correspondent banking (SWIFT sees the whole flow map).
2. **The legal test reads content, not mechanism.** Antitrust bans the output
   shape regardless of leakage (RealPage/Agri Stats); ITAR makes ingress the
   export before any annotation; UFLPA demands raw ordinary-course docs; NVRA
   forces record-level reveal as the endgame; MNPI-derived annotation is still
   MNPI. Metering entropy defends against the wrong thing.
3. **The commodity *is* the harm.** When the deliverable's few load-bearing bits
   are the secret, bit-accounting is decorative and the budget either blocks the
   work or funds the leak. *Fired on:* semiconductor recipe root-cause, one M&A
   change-of-control clause, one CBRN jailbreak, the affected-set in fraud
   rings. (This is harm-nonlinear-in-bits biting.)
4. **The incumbent already ships the claimed delta.** Decentriq runs arbitrary
   counterparty code over top-sensitivity data; AWS Clean Rooms DP is a composing
   budget; OverWatch does open-ended cross-customer hunting; YCharOS does open
   orthogonal revalidation; geo-experiments give platform-independent
   incrementality.
5. **Revealed preference / dead market.** Willingness-to-pay, not privacy
   machinery, was the missing input. IronNet died under 100 customers; Ocean
   Protocol Compute-to-Data shipped the exact crossing and got no supply; ERIC
   members balked at dues, not cryptography.
6. **Frontier-leaning safety, asserted away.** The protection rests on an
   unsolved canon frontier (verification-as-extraction, no-auxiliary-knowledge,
   doubly-sealed verifier, extra-substrate enforcement, human-head channel) and
   pretends it is solved. *Fired on:* model-eval exchange, litigation
   source-review, training-data-rights, defense co-engineering.
7. **Open-endedness is decorative / the union is not actually invisible.** The
   "only visible in the pooled union" premise is often empirically false
   (a single sportsbook caught the Clase/Ortiz betting ring; satellite burns
   broadcast to the sky; the patent corpus is public), and where a fixed query
   family suffices, "open-ended agent cognition" is a small query family in
   disguise — and open-endedness often *maximizes* the extraction fear that
   motivates siloing in the first place.

> **Meta-lesson.** A plausible domain dies not because the substrate is weak but
> because the substrate's genuine strength — composition-charged egress metering
> over open-ended third-party cognition — is answering a question that domain
> does not ask.

### The four survivors

All four share one crossing shape: **a low-bit typed verdict emitted over
high-bit sealed data owned by a party who is often not the Chamber owner, where
the sanctioned output *is* the exfiltration channel** — verification-as-
extraction as the central, not incidental, threat. That is precisely the point
where composition-charged egress metering (EgressBudget + CompositionState,
adversarial-max charging, absence/timing/refusal as emissions) stops being
decorative and becomes the whole answer. Each pairs it with a regulatory/
catastrophe clock and an incumbent that cannot scale to machine speed or cannot
preserve singleton/forensic fidelity.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Third-party security research over sealed vendor artifacts** (vuln discovery, CVD/CRA blast-radius, detection-rule bounties) | **68** | Composition-aware egress metering on the research agent's *own* typed outputs — CC protects the workload from the host, but here the workload is the adversary and its sanctioned output (repro, VEX, detection rule) is the exfil channel. | **D2** | **Open-ended cognition over sealed patient-level health data** (trial reanalysis, RWE epidemiology, misconduct forensics, cross-manufacturer pharmacovigilance) | **60** | A per-observer accountant metering the *full* emission surface (incl. absence/timing/refusal) over exploratory third-party queries while still permitting typed *non-aggregate* forensic verdicts — the point DP (destroys subgroup fidelity) and clean rooms (pre-agreed queries only) cannot occupy. | FDA/EMA post-market; ~15% IPD availability gate |
| **D3** | **Genomic discovery & rare-variant matchmaking over re-identifiable genotype silos** (biobanks + post-bankruptcy consumer-genome custody) | **49** | A priced composition accountant over heterogeneous adversarial probes in the **singleton regime where DP structurally collapses**, plus consent that survives a custody transfer as a salted tombstone. | **D4** | **Confidential expert review of unpublished work** (paid peer review + dual-use hazard screening) | **47** | The reviewer's *agent* reviews inside the author's chamber so the raw blueprint never egresses to a competitor's head; a doubly-sealed non-disclosing hazard verdict; the only priority artifact possible for unpublishable work. | NIH ban on AI in peer review; scoop-prone fields |

[`memo-2-sealed-health-data.md`](memo-2-sealed-health-data.md),

**Two clusters, two singletons.** *Sealed patient/genotype data* (D2+D3) share
the most missing primitives and both lean hardest on the auxiliary-knowledge
frontier — D3 concedes its central control fails against an attacker holding a
reference panel plus the target's own genotype, which is why the what-breaks
judge ranks it last. *Hazard-screen-over-IP* (D1+D4) both emit a non-disclosing
verdict where the evaluator itself is an attackable oracle, and both must name
harm-nonlinear-in-bits honestly rather than assert it away. **D1 is the run's
strongest on every economic axis and depends on no new frontier item beyond the
incumbent's own** — it is where to start.

### Aggregate primitive pressure on the canon

Counted by recurrence across survivors, separating universal need from clean
buildability (many high-recurrence needs fail prong-3 and stay frontier-bound):

1. **Non-owner authority over an emission/verdict/consent — 4/4.** The party
   governing what may be emitted is frequently *not* the single `Chamber.ownerId`
   (the sealed-telemetry owner; a non-party FDA/ORI adjudicator; the data
   subject; a biosafety officer). Aggregate pressure on **open-frontier #8
   (multi-owner authority)** — the #1 change pressure. *Clean admissible subset:*
   `SealedEvalGrant` and `SubjectConsentScope` (both pass all five prongs);
   `PanelCountersignature` only as a pointer+quorum wrapper; `OwnerQuorum` /
   `DualControlEscalationAuthority` **fail Composition and stay frontier.**
2. **Composition-accountant extensions beyond a static `subjectHash` — 4/4.**
   Composition over a *mutating* subject (successive builds; the leaked quantity
   is the diff), calibrated against a *named auxiliary reference panel* (not
   absolute bits), over *refusals* (which reconstruct a portfolio), over *verdict
   deltas* (which reconstruct the oracle). Highest-confidence changes because
   they are **additive extensions to the substrate's actual built strength** — a
   lineage-aware `subjectHash`, a calibration record — not new modules. Build
   these first.
3. **Reconstruction-of-the-oracle guard — 3/4.** The verdict channel is itself
   attackable (TP/FP deltas → sealed telemetry; screen deltas → the red-line
   boundary; allele-frequency deltas → Homer-style identification). Shared
   machinery across the security and patient clusters; build once, reuse.
4. **Absence/refusal as an owner-reserved metered emission — ~3/4.**
   `entropy.ts` has `ObservableKind "absence"` but **no `"refusal"`** — a
   cheap, high-value schema addition so a true-zero and a deliberate decline
   emit through one padded channel at one fixed bit (`PresenceReserve`).
5. **Non-aggregate single-record forensic warrant vs. the denominator guard —
   2/4** (patient cluster). A typed n=1 verdict must cross `DenominatorGuard`,
   debited at full re-identification cost, authorized by a named authority.
6. **Non-bit hazard/weaponization verdict — 2/4** (hazard cluster). A release
   gate on an axis *orthogonal* to the bit accountant — explicitly a
   frontier-*naming* record for #4, never a solver.
7–8. **Domain-local (1 each):** `CrossChamberRecordLinkage` (matching over
   records not persons, D2); `CustodyTransfer` (whole-Chamber authority handoff
   with obligation inheritance, D3).

> **Net:** two universal additive extensions — composition-key generalization +
> the two admissible non-owner-authority records — carry ~¾ of the value.
> Everything else is cluster-local or frontier-bound.

### Build sequence

- **Phase 0 — canon extensions first (unblock everything).** (a) Generalize
  `CompositionKey` to carry a lineage/mutating-subject dimension and
  auxiliary-knowledge calibration (subsumes D1's `ArtifactLineageCompositionKey`
  and D3's `ReconstructionCapacityBound` as configurations of one record);
  (b) add `"refusal"` to `entropy.ts` `ObservableKind`. Additive, fail no prong,
  needed by every survivor.
- **Phase 1 — D1 Security.** Wins on all three economic axes and both
  non-degenerate judges. Existing priced bounty market, a fine-indexed CRA clock,
  a loop that recurs by construction, safety resting entirely on built
  egress-metering strength. Also the cleanest place to validate `SealedEvalGrant`
  (the strongest clearly-missing primitive in the whole run) and the
  oracle-reconstruction guard — building D1 forges the machinery Phase 3 needs.
- **Phase 2 — patient-cluster shared primitives.** `PresenceReserve` (enabled by
  Phase 0) and `NonAggregateForensicWarrant`, built once for D2 and D3.
- **Phase 3a — D2 Health.** Highest stakes; sequenced behind D1 on slow
  institutional procurement and on frontier #8 (named via `PanelCountersignature`,
  not solved). Deploy as a regulator/HTA pilot. Add `CrossChamberRecordLinkage`.
- **Phase 3b — D3 Genomic**, behind an explicit frontier-naming gate. Reuses
  Phase 2; adds `SubjectConsentScope` + `CustodyTransfer`. Ships **only** with
  `ReconstructionCapacityBound` recording "UNSAFE if attacker holds the target's
  own genotype." A responsible owner may rationally refuse to deploy — treat that
  as a feature of the gate.
- **Phase 4 (optional) — D4 Peer review.** Cheap rider on D1's oracle-
  reconstruction machinery, but plausibly-negative unit economics; build as a
  funded public-goods pilot, not a revenue bet.

## Open questions worth their own memos

1. **Multi-owner authority (frontier #8)** — the run's most recurrent unsolved
   need (4/4). Is there an admissible `QuorumDecision` gating `ownerDecision`
   that adds no new emission surface, or does every version smuggle in the
   proof-of-uniqueness assumption (#1) the canon cannot back?
2. **Auxiliary-knowledge model (frontier #5)** — can composition be bounded
   relative to a named, versioned reference panel honestly enough to deploy D3,
   and what is the correct receipt language for "safe only against attackers
   weaker than panel P"? The line between deployable-with-warning and negligent.
3. **Harm-nonlinear-in-bits (frontier #4)** — is a non-bit release gate a
   coherent primitive, or does it merely relocate an irreducible human judgment
   into a typed wrapper? Low-hazard fragments composing into a full weapon is the
   open sub-problem.
4. **Verification-cost economics (frontier #14)** — at what verification premium
   does each market stop clearing, and can the premium be priced into the bounty
   rather than eaten by the owner?
5. **Is open-endedness actually load-bearing?** — give an operational test
   distinguishing genuine open-ended agent cognition from a fixed query family
   in disguise, and re-audit each survivor against it. Highest-leverage re-audit:
   if a survivor's value is really a small query family, PSI/clean-rooms may
   already dominate it.
6. **Worker-side fair exchange (frontier #2)** — does oracle-driven acceptance
   actually remove counterparty read-then-reject discretion, or relocate it into
   oracle capture?
7. **Forensic-warrant authority** — who may authorize an n=1 crossing of the
   denominator guard, and does that authority reduce to the same unsolved
   multi-owner problem as (1)?
8. **Cross-run re-audit as standing practice** — the forensic memo's biggest
   catches came from later runs refuting earlier runs' blessed claims. No run has
   yet code-judged *these four survivors'* load-bearing claims against the
   modules. Schedule it — treat this atlas as the earlier run to be refuted.

## Canon posture

Nothing here amends `primitives/CANON.md`. Every proposed record and law is a
*proposal* carrying its admission-test verdict; every declined primitive is
routed to the named open frontier rather than asserted away. If any of these are
adopted, they enter through the five-prong gate explicitly — the same discipline
the doctrine demands of the research itself.

## Addendum — Fable 5 review (2026-07-02, added post-hoc)

A Fable-5-high-effort session re-read `CANON.md` and the modules and delivered
the sharpest single objection to this cut, accepted here:

> *You decorrelated the generators and then killed with one correlated,
> leakage-shaped, kill-defaulted judge — violating your own Rule 1 at exactly
> the stage Rule 4 calls the deliverable — so the atlas measured the substrate
> against its adversarial/egress-metering half and never against its
> cooperative/market/attribution half, then reported the result as "which
> economies the substrate unlocks."*

Three consequences, recorded rather than buried:

1. **The 88% kill rate is inflated.** Incumbent *parity claims* were taken at
   face value (often from a vendor's own marketing) and never adversarially
   verified the way survivor findings were. The kills need a symmetric
   resurrection pass; treat 88% as an upper bound until they get one.

2. **The honest survivor headline is not "4 survived."** By the run's own text
   it is **"1 strong (D1 security research), 1 frontier-gated (D2 sealed health
   data), 2 marginal (D3 genomic — near-dead by prong 6, 'a responsible owner
   may rationally refuse to deploy'; D4 peer review — 'plausibly-negative unit
   economics', routed to public-goods pilot)."** D1 is where adversarial framing
   and a real market genuinely coincide.

3. **The screen is blind to half the canon.** It can only see the
   adversarial/leakage organ. The substrate has a second genuine strength —
   `market.ts` reuse-edges / attribution / credit-settlement, the ESTIMATED lane
   and heterogeneous valuation in `iptrade.ts` — built for **cooperative**
   economies where the binding constraint is *credit/attribution/composition*,
   not leakage. Every such economy dies on prong 1 ("parties trust each other →
   sovereignty not load-bearing") — which is the screen looking through the
   wrong organ, not deadness. Also structurally absent: **individual/consumer-
   scale** economies (the `matching.ts` "dating" + attention-pricing half — the
   canon's personal-sovereignty origin, which the atlas silently narrowed away
   from), and **worker-side** economies where the private asset is the *agent's
   method*, not the owner's data (frontier #2).

**The prescribed fix (a follow-on cut, `2026-07-02-cooperative-economy-atlas`
and a kill-resurrection pass):** (a) run the gauntlet's skepticism *symmetrically*
on the 28 kills' incumbent-parity claims, resurrecting false kills to the
survivor-audit standard; (b) run one generator pass over the cooperative /
attribution / personal-sovereignty / worker-side half of the canon with a
**decorrelated, non-leakage** judge panel (credit-defaulted and demand-defaulted
skeptics, not one leakage-defaulted killer). This is what this cut's own doctrine
prescribed and skipped.
