# Genomic discovery & rare-variant matchmaking over re-identifiable genotype silos

## 1. The silo bind and who must cooperate

A genotype is the one datum you cannot rotate. Biobanks, hospital genomics units, and consumer-genome custodians each hold patient-level variant call sets keyed to phenotype — data that is maximally re-identifying, non-revocable, and consented per-cohort. Three facts make this a substrate problem rather than a data-sharing problem.

First, the value and the harm are the same fact. Confirming that a novel ultra-rare variant is causal requires finding the *one other carrier on Earth* — a query whose honest answer ("yes, one person") is, in a small named cohort, roughly `log2(N_population)` ≈ 30+ bits of identification about a single living person. The signal a researcher needs and the re-identification an attacker wants are the same bit.

Second, the incumbent controls are provably broken here. GA4GH Beacon answers a fixed binary "is this allele present?" and defends itself with a per-user *access budget* — count the queries, cut you off. That budget does not compose: the 2024–2025 **Beacon Reconstruction Attack** (Saleem, Cicek & Sav, *Bioinformatics* 2025) shows that beacons *releasing allele frequencies* let an optimizer reconstruct participant genomes at F1 ≈ 0.70 vs 0.45 baseline. Differential privacy — the abstract owner of "composition math" — structurally collapses at count ≈ 1: you cannot add enough noise to hide a singleton without destroying the singleton. MPC and clean rooms answer only *pre-agreed* queries, and frontier discovery is open-ended hypothesis search. Matchmaker Exchange (120K+ cases) works but is cooperative, non-adversarial, and non-monetized — a goodwill network among collaborators, not a metered market among rivals.

Third, the exit that would have paid for all this is closed. 23andMe filed Chapter 11 in March 2025; a coalition of **27 states plus DC** sued to block transfer of ~15M genotypes without explicit consent, and the assets ultimately went to **TTAM Research Institute for $305M (closed July 2025)** under binding privacy commitments that must survive any *further* transfer of ownership. Sell-the-database monetization is dead. What remains is per-crossing revenue and consent that survives custody handoff — and no deployed system provides it.

**Who must cooperate:** mutually distrusting rivals, not consortium members. Competing pharma/biotech discovery units and independent clinical researchers, each running their own GWAS/PheWAS/variant-burden hypotheses across each other's cohorts; clinician case-submitters with a single undiagnosed patient; and — post-bankruptcy — custodians who now must earn per query because they can no longer sell the vault.

## 2. The crossing, end-to-end, in canonical nouns

**Parties (fictional, realistic).** *Dr. Amara Okonkwo*, a pediatric neurologist at a children's hospital, has one patient carrying a novel de-novo missense variant in *KCNT2* and a specific seizure phenotype. She is backed by the **Halvorsen Epilepsy Foundation** (the buyer). Three silos may hold the second carrier: **Meridian BioVault** (a TTAM-pattern consumer-genome custodian), **Cascade Health Genomics** (a hospital biobank), and **Nordreach Population Bank** (a national biobank).

1. Each custodian instantiates a **Chamber** (`ownerId` = custodian `Principal`, `defaultRelease` = `review_required`, `RetentionPolicy.disputeHorizon` outliving every discovery **RunClaim**). The cohort's variant calls + phenotypes become a **Scope** (`sensitivity: "special"`, `requesterVisibleClasses: ["medical"]`, `presentation: "aggregate_only"`, `canaryHash` seeding a synthetic phantom carrier to catch reserve/reconstruction probing).

2. The custodian publishes an **EnvRecipe** over variant-presence + phenotype-bucket views: **MountSpec** `access: "synthetic_preview"` / `pathVirtualization: "opaque"`, **NetworkPolicy** `mode: "none"` (`rawPrivateDataMayTransit: false`), **ModelAccessPolicy** `trainingUseForbidden: true`, **SinkPolicy** `durableChannel: "typed_annotation"` with a small `maxCapacityBits`. The **AgentPackage** is the pinned matching agent (`bundleHash`, `noMutableRemoteCode: true`).

3. The Foundation files a **Transform**: `declaredPurpose: "KCNT2 missense carrier match"`, `untrustedPromptHash` over the query variant+phenotype (requester input is untrusted), **InputPolicy** `allowedGranularity: "search_hit"` / `canRevealSourceLocators: false`, **OutputPolicy** (`freeText: "forbidden"`, `exactCountsAllowed: false`, `allowedFieldClasses: ["boolean","bucket","enum"]`).

4. The custodian issues a **Grant** (`granteeId` = worker agent, `allowedScopeIds` = [cohort Scope], `envRecipeId`, `sink`). No Grant, no Run.

5. A **CompositionKey** is derived (`subjectHash` over the carrier partition, `queryFamilyHash` = the KCNT2 association family, `audienceHash` = Foundation **BeneficialEntity**, `windowHash`). An **EgressBudget** opens for observer `"requester"`, scope `"query_family"`, `repeatedQueryComposition: "blocked"`, ceilings set from a reconstruction capacity bound.

6. The **Run** advances `submit → static_scan → preflight → owner_execution → worker → release_review → owner_disclosure`. The worker emits an **Annotation** (`role: "match_candidate"`) whose typed payload is `{presence: boolean, phenotypeConcordance: bucket, effectSize: bucket, cohortDenominator: bucket}` carrying a **LeakageEstimate**.

7. Every observable — the presence bit, the *absence* (no carrier), timing, refusal-on-exhaustion — is an **ObservableEvent** under an **ObservablePolicy** + **ObfuscationPlan** (`absenceIsAnEmission`). A **CapacityEstimate** charges at the adversarial maximum (`enumValueBits + orderingBits + fieldPresenceBits`) under an **EstimatorAttestation** (`independence: "role_separated"`, `worstCaseOverSecrets: true`). An **EgressDebit** debits; **CompositionState.cumulativeStructuredBits** increments toward `structuredBitsCeiling`; the `releaseGate` is the *conjunction* of the numeric accountant and the **EntropyReview** ordinal gate.

8. A **DenominatorGuard** (`minGroupSize`, `zeroOneManyOnly`, `suppressWhenExternallyIdentifiable: true`) fires: a count = 1 carrier is the singleton regime. A bare "yes, exactly one" is maximally re-identifying, so it is suppressed out of the free-probe channel and forced into a priced, consent-gated relation.

9. Because a two-carrier hit is a relation between two sovereign worlds (Okonkwo's patient Chamber and the custodian's cohort Chamber), it is a **CandidateRelation** (`matching.ts`): `scoreBucket`, owner-private `rationaleArtifactId`, `denominatorGuardId`, **ConsentPolicy** (`denialVisibleToCounterparty: false`), **BilateralReleasePolicy** (`releaseMode: "mediated"`, `revealScore: "bucket_only"`).

10. Pricing: the Foundation commits a bid **PriceDistribution**; the custodian an ask + **ReservePrice**; both post **CoinCommitments**; a steward sampler (`samplerMayNotBeAParty`) draws **PriceSamples**; a **PriceCross** clears iff bid-draw ≥ ask-draw ≥ reserve. `counterpartyLearns: "one_bit"`. A `no_cross` is an absence emission worth one bit that *still debits composition* (probing reserves is a reconstruction attack). The cleared cross authorizes the CandidateRelation to surface.

11. Escalation to clinician-mediated contact or carrier identity is content disclosure: a **Release** with non-empty `reviewerIds` and a *human* `ownerDecision`. A **ReviewCard** reaches the custodian steward via **AttentionQueue** under an **AttentionBudget** (`failClosedForDisclosure`).

12. Receipts: a **ReceiptPayload** with bucketed `visibleClaims`, caveats `["bounded_leakage","not_anonymity"]`, `noPerfectSecrecyClaim: true`; a **VerificationVerdict** partitioning *proven* ("a carrier is present under the TEE vendor root") / *trusted* / *unprovable* ("this variant is causal") — never a boolean `verified`; a **PlainAccount** stating whatCrossed / whatDidNotCross / whatItCannotPromise.

For open-ended discovery (variant-burden GWAS rather than a singleton match), a **NegotiationLane** links buyer and custodian Chambers with **RevealStages** gated on **EscrowedClaims** (`claimClass: "dataset_property"`, `verificationPlan: "tee_replication"`), per-party EgressBudgets — verification precedes valuation.

## 3. Entropy-budget economics and the composition story

What leaks per probe, charged adversarially: a presence bit is 1 bit honest, but a "present" for an ultra-rare variant in a small named cohort is ~30+ bits about one carrier — one catastrophic attribute, one life (canon frontier #4: harm is not linear in bits). Allele-frequency probes leak the count, which against a reference panel (gnomAD/1000G) is the **Homer-2008** likelihood-ratio channel — precisely what the Beacon Reconstruction Attack exploited to reach F1 0.70. Phenotype-concordance annotations leak genotype *and* cohort membership jointly; bucketed effect-size over tiny n leaks the underlying counts.

So `CapacityEstimate` charges `enumValueBits + orderingBits + fieldPresenceBits` at the adversarial maximum, and `CompositionState` sums them monotonically against `structuredBitsCeiling`. This *is* the principled successor to Beacon's per-user budget, which counted accesses, not bits, and thus composed straight into a reconstruction. Rough numbers: a match schema of `{presence, phenotypeConcordance(4 buckets), effectSize(4 buckets), denominator(zero/one/many)}` is ~1 + 2 + 2 + 1.6 ≈ 7 adversarial-max bits per annotation. Set `structuredBitsCeiling` at, say, 40 bits against a named panel and a query family exhausts after ~5–6 heterogeneous probes — long before an optimizer accumulates enough constraints to reconstruct. A budget buys a *bounded* number of structured probes whose cumulative bits keep the attacker's posterior identification LR below a re-ID threshold *given a named reference panel* — a tripwire (`budgetsAreTripwiresNotCertificates`), never a certificate.

In the singleton regime DP collapses, so `DenominatorGuard` + suppression + the priced-mediated-relation path *replaces* noise as the only honest control. What a crossing costs: a confirmed second carrier is worth six-to-seven figures to a rare-disease program (target validation + avoided cohort recruitment), so the PriceCross should clear well above the custodian's ReservePrice — and the reserve must exceed the *marginal, irreplaceable* re-ID capacity the custodian permanently spends. Each probe consumes finite re-ID capacity against that cohort: a composition rent that rises as the budget depletes. The buyer pays per crossing because both alternatives are dead — buying the database is AG-blocked, and Beacon's fixed yes/no cannot answer open-ended search. Absence and refusal are priced emissions, so a rival cannot probe for free by reading silence.

## 4. Feedback loop and licensing/royalty design

The Foundation posts a standing **MatchBounty** (find carriers across a KCNT2 variant panel) with an **AcceptanceRule** and **CreditPool**. Each candidate-match Annotation becomes an **Acceptance** scored by a pinned **EvaluatorOracle** — a versioned ACMG/pathogenicity classifier checkpoint + `rubricHash`, its author role-separated from any paid worker via **ConflictOfInterestCheck** over BeneficialEntities (`anOracleUpgradeIsANewOracle`). A **SettlementPayoutAuthorization** (one human act, bound before work) then settles the custodian's per-match payout *zero-touch* within `perPayoutCeiling`/`windowCeiling`, while the actual clinician-mediated *contact* still requires a per-Release human `ownerDecision` — money and disclosure deliberately split.

The loop compounds: a confirmed two-carrier concordance raises that variant's pathogenicity score, which lifts the **PriceSchedule** payout for the *next* carrier, which pulls more custodians into publishing EnvRecipes — an oracle-reinforced recruitment flywheel, not a one-shot trade. A standing **AnnotationFeed** + **FeedSubscription** (`reviewBeforeNotify`) surfaces newly-enrolled matching carriers over time; **ReuseEdges** + **AttributionShare** route **CreditSettlement** back to both custodian and original clinician when a discovered cohort later feeds a pharma's target-validation Run.

Licensing is consent-first, evidence-as-deterrent. The cohort is a **TradedAsset** (`carrier: "curated_dataset"`) under a **LicenseGrant** bound *before* work (`priceScheduleId`, `payoutAuthorizationId`, `auditRights: "attestation_only"`). When a licensee reuses a discovered cohort downstream, it self-issues a signed **ReuseAttestation** (in-toto/DSSE) triggering the schedule — the substrate does not surveil the licensee's premises. Silent pooling is raised as **ReuseEvidence** (`method: "statistical_similarity"`, with `pValue`, `falsePositiveBound`, and a `rebutted_independent_discovery` state — because a variant-phenotype association can be genuinely independently discovered). A bare hypothesis traded as technique is `pure_recipe` and pins to `unprovable`. Trust roots degrade loudly: verification rides `tee_vendor_root`; a **SlashableBond** carries `consequenceRoot: "jurisdictional_arbitration"` because the AG consent decree / TTAM commitment is the real extra-substrate enforcement.

## 5. The wedge: first customer, first 90 days, what is billed

**First customer:** the Halvorsen Epilepsy Foundation, matching one KCNT2 variant across Meridian BioVault + Cascade + Nordreach. Not a consortium package — one foundation's cohort inside one custodian Chamber, with one buyer running variant-burden discovery under an entropy budget, priced per query. This replaces the AG-blocked "sell the whole database" model with the only monetization a post-23andMe custodian can defend to its regulator.

**First 90 days.** Days 1–30: stand up one custodian Chamber, publish one EnvRecipe over one cohort, wire the DenominatorGuard + a single query-family EgressBudget against a named gnomAD version. Days 31–60: run Okonkwo's KCNT2 match end-to-end — Grant, Run, match_candidate Annotation, DenominatorGuard suppression, PriceCross, mediated CandidateRelation, human ownerDecision contact gate, receipt with proven/unprovable partition. Days 61–90: turn on the MatchBounty + PriceSchedule + zero-touch SettlementPayoutAuthorization so the second and third carriers settle without re-litigating each disclosure.

**What is billed.** (a) A per-crossing fee at each cleared PriceCross — the atomic revenue event. (b) A composition-rent premium as the query-family budget depletes (later probes cost more). (c) A standing bounty subscription for the AnnotationFeed of newly-enrolled matching carriers. (d) Escalation-to-contact as a separately-gated, separately-priced disclosure. The custodian is paid for the *look* and the *match*, never for the cohort.

## 6. Missing primitives (admission-test verdicts, proposed as canon changes)

Three genuine gaps; each passes all five admission tests and is proposed as a first-class record, never drift.

**(1) `SubjectConsentScope`** (`subjectHash → allowed query families`, revocable). The load-bearing gap. `Chamber.ownerId` is one custodian Principal, but the data belongs to millions of subjects who are not Principals in any Run yet hold consent + erasure rights the custodian only stewards. `matching.ts` `ConsentRecord` binds a Principal *member* of one CandidateRelation; it cannot gate every ObservableEvent touching a subject across all query families, nor express "consented to presence but not phenotype-concordance." *Boundary:* names "may this subject's genotype join this query family," today folklore. *Lifecycle:* created at enrollment, revoked at opt-out, revocation retroactively suppresses future probes. *Composition:* a narrowly-consenting subject must not have broad probes composed against them. *Owner:* separates custodian authority from subject authority. *Receipt:* "queried only over consented subjects; bucketed N opted out." PASSES. Honest caveat: does not solve multi-owner authority (#8) or proof-of-uniqueness (#1).

**(2) `CustodyTransfer`** (whole-Chamber authority handoff with obligation inheritance). `Chamber.ownerId` is immutable; `ErasureRequest` is per-artifact. The 23andMe → Regeneron → TTAM pattern demands migrating every salted tombstone, *freezing and carrying* the EgressBudget/CompositionState (else the new owner offers a fresh budget an adversary re-queries), and binding the new owner to the prior consent envelope without widening it. *Boundary:* "authority over this silo passed A→B and B is bound by X" — today an AG consent decree with no computational form. *Lifecycle:* created at APA close, audited against the decree. *Composition:* the transfer must not reset composition accounting. PASSES. Distinct from ErasureRequest and Grant revocation.

**(3) `ReconstructionCapacityBound`** (aux-knowledge calibration for the EgressBudget). The delta claims CompositionState is "tuned to the reconstruction-attack capacity bound," but `CapacityEstimate` charges bits worst-case over *schema* — absolute bits, not bits relative to an attacker holding a reference panel plus the target's own genotype (the Homer / Shringarpure-Bustamante channel). Candidate: a record naming the assumed reference panel + attacker prior against which `structuredBitsCeiling` is set, re-versioned as panels grow. PASSES all five, *but must name canon frontier #5* ("confidence bounded by the assumed prior"), never assert it solved.

**Rejected:** (a) `AlleleFrequencyProbe` — a payload schema + `ObservableKind: "exact_count"` bucketed, already carried by Annotation + DenominatorGuard; fails Boundary/Lifecycle standalone. (b) `ClinicianContactChannel` — already a Release gated by ownerDecision + matching's mediated `firstMessageSchemaId`; the only novelty (contacting a non-Principal subject) folds into `SubjectConsentScope`.

## 7. Open-frontier dependencies and what breaks

This domain leans hardest on five unsolved frontier items. **#5 (no auxiliary-knowledge model):** budgets are absolute bits, not bits relative to the observer's prior — so the ceiling is only as sound as the named panel, and an attacker who already holds the target's genotype defeats it. `ReconstructionCapacityBound` *names* this; it does not close it. **#4 (harm nonlinear in bits):** one presence bit can be one life. The DenominatorGuard is a coarse proxy; the accountant cannot see that this bit is catastrophic. **#8 (multi-owner authority):** millions of subjects behind one custodian Principal is exactly the quorum/role gap `SubjectConsentScope` bounds but does not solve. **#9 (purpose enforcement):** `declaredPurpose` is an unenforced string — a biobank's IRB may refuse to admit *any* open-ended third-party cognition regardless of substrate, which caps the wedge to cohorts whose consent already permits it. **#1 (proof-of-uniqueness):** a rival could split into two probing units to double a budget; `ConflictOfInterestCheck` relocates but does not decide this. And **#11–12 (method verification / verification-as-extraction):** causality is `unprovable`, and the query access that proves a carrier exists is itself a channel that leaks.

## 8. Honest kill-risks

1. **Aggregate-discovery erosion.** The open-ended GWAS half is largely covered by DP + clean rooms and is decorative. Only the *singleton + adversarial-rival + custody-survival* slice truly survives — a narrow wedge, not a broad platform.
2. **Purpose/IRB gate is extra-substrate.** Frontier #9 means a biobank may simply refuse open-ended third-party queries. If governance says no, the substrate never runs.
3. **Demand risk.** Rival pharma may prefer bilateral MME/clean-room deals, or outright data acquisition where legally available, over a shared metered substrate they do not control.
4. **Aux-knowledge collapse.** If attackers routinely hold reference panels near the target, the honest ceiling shrinks toward zero and the product must say `unsafe` — killing the crossing it exists to sell.
5. **Enforcement is not the receipt.** Cryptographic receipts are not self-enforcing contracts; the whole consent-through-custody promise rests on `jurisdictional_arbitration` and an AG decree the substrate cannot execute.
