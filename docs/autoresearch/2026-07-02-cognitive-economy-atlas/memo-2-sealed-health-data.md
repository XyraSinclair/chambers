# Sealed Patient Data, Open-Ended Cognition: The Composition-Accountant Wedge

## 1. The silo bind and who must cooperate

Patient-level health knowledge is stranded by construction. Sponsors hold individual patient data (IPD) from registrational trials; hospitals and HMOs hold OMOP-mapped EHR and claims; academic labs hold the original figures behind their papers; and each manufacturer holds its own adverse-event (AE) case database of raw MedDRA-coded narratives. Every one of these is simultaneously HIPAA/GDPR-regulated, competitively sensitive, and frequently contractually un-shareable. The consortium bureaus that are supposed to fix this — WHO VigiBase, EMA EudraVigilance, FDA FAERS — receive only de-identified, fixed-schema ICSRs. The rich raw substrate never leaves the holder.

The cost of the bind is not abstract. A rising safety trend that is real but fragmented across four manufacturers' case files never clears the disproportionality threshold in any single database, so a label change arrives late, after avoidable deaths. A manipulated Western blot is a $15M False Claims Act exposure — the December 2025 Dana-Farber settlement is now the reference price, with the outside sleuth who surfaced the images reportedly collecting a multimillion-dollar relator's share. And IPD "availability" through Vivli/YODA-style bureaus is anemic: sharing *declarations* run high, but actual per-trial IPD access remains a minority event (COVID-pharmacology IPD was accessible for 22% of RCTs, and unrestricted availability is lower still). Roughly ~15% is the working number the field quotes for practical IPD reuse.

Who must cooperate is a set of mutually distrusting parties: independent replication and meta-analysis researchers; journal integrity officers; funders and oversight bodies (NIH, ORI, HTA agencies like NICE/ICER); regulators (FDA/EMA); and — the sharpest edge — **direct commercial rivals** in pharmacovigilance who share no case-level trust root whatsoever. The reason cooperation fails is not missing goodwill. It is that a data holder cannot *bound* what an open-ended external reanalysis will leak, so the rational move is refusal. The ~15% gate is a leakage-bounding failure wearing a governance costume.

## 2. The crossing, end to end, in canonical nouns

Take the hardest case: a cross-manufacturer safety-signal reanalysis. Two rivals — **Meridian Therapeutics** (drug: *velestat*) and **Corvane Pharma** (drug: *daporsen*, same class) — plus an **FDA** post-market safety officer suspect a shared class signal for a rare hepatic event, MedDRA PT "hepatic failure acute." Neither company will tell the other whether it even *has* cases for that term; that presence bit is competitive intelligence.

Each rival's AE database is a `Chamber` (`ownerId` = the firm's safety-org `Principal`, `defaultRelease: "review_required"`, `retention.disputeHorizon` spanning the regulatory dispute window). Inside each, the narratives are a `Scope`: `requesterVisibleClasses: ["medical"]`, `sensitivity: "special"`, `presentation: "aggregate_only"`, `selectorHash` pinning the (drug, event-term) query family, `canaryHash` seeded for exfiltration detection.

An independent PV agent (`Principal` role `worker_agent`) is admitted into **both** chambers, each under its own `Grant` (`grantorId` = that owner). The `EnvRecipe` sets `IsolationMode: "tee_attested"` — NVIDIA Confidential Computing seals the raw narratives — with `MountSpec` `read_only` + `pathVirtualization: "opaque"`, `NetworkPolicy: "none"`, and `SinkPolicy.durableChannel: "typed_annotation"`. This is, by canon's own admission, the *doubly-sealed verifier* (open frontier #13): one agent sees both rivals' secrets. Named hazard, not solved problem.

The query enters as a `Transform`: `requesterId` = FDA, `declaredPurpose` = "class disproportionality reanalysis for hepatic failure acute" (an unenforced string — frontier #9). `InputPolicy.allowedGranularity: "aggregate"`, `canRevealSourceLocators: false`. `OutputPolicy.freeText: "forbidden"`, `exactCountsAllowed: false`, `sourceListsAllowed: false`, `allowedFieldClasses: ["bucket","enum"]`. The `Run` advances through `Gate`s — `submit → static_scan → preflight → owner_execution → worker → release_review → owner_disclosure` — every transition a `LedgerEntry`.

The agent emits `Annotation`s: role `risk_flag` carrying a bucketed ROR/PRR, role `correction` for probable-duplicate flags. Each carries a `LeakageEstimate` whose `EstimatorAttestation.independence` must **not** be `self_interested` — a paid agent that understates its own side-channel bits silently breaks every budget (`ENTROPY_LAWS.everyEstimateNamesItsEstimator`).

The metering is the crux. A `CompositionKey` binds `subjectHash` = (drug, term), `queryFamilyHash`, `audienceHash` = (FDA, rival-Corvane), `windowHash`. A per-observer `EgressBudget` (`observer: "sponsor"`, `scope: "query_family"`) governs. Each answer produces `ObservableEvent`s: `answer_field` (which ROR bucket), `absence` (does Meridian hold ≥1 case), `timing` (when it became emittable). `CapacityEstimate` charges at the **adversarial maximum**: `enumValueBits + orderingBits + fieldPresenceBits`, because a malicious agent steganographically encodes a specific patient in the *choice* of bucket and the *ordering* of flags. `EgressDebit`s sum into `CompositionState.cumulativeStructuredBits`, monotone against `structuredBitsCeiling`. The `DenominatorGuard` sets `minGroupSize`, `zeroOneManyOnly: true`, `suppressWhenExternallyIdentifiable: true` — rare AE × rare indication reconstructs a patient. `releaseGate` is the **conjunction** of `numericAccountantTripped` and an ordinal `EntropyReview`. Repeated presence-probes across the MedDRA tree trip `repeated_query_reconstruction` and block — this is verification-as-extraction (frontier #12) made a typed, refusable event.

To pool the two contributions, the chambers open a `NegotiationLane` (`partyChamberIds: [Meridian, Corvane]`, `mediatorId` = a neutral steward, `symmetryRule: "strict_reciprocal"`). Each bounded contribution is committed as an `EscrowedClaim` before reveal, disclosed up a `RevealStage` ladder with **per-party** `EgressBudget` (no shared budget to hide behind). Disclosure is priced: each owner posts a `PriceDistribution` (ask) and a `ReservePrice` with `purposeDisclosure: "none_required"` — paid for the look without admitting a problem — and a jointly-committed coin drives a `PriceSample`; the `PriceCross` outcome (`cleared`/`no_cross`, `counterpartyLearns: "one_bit"`) debits the same `CompositionState`.

The trust root is named and degrades loudly: `TrustRoot.kind` = `tee_vendor_root` (the enclave) plus `single_ttp` (the FDA-operated enclave host), `degradesTo: "explicit_unprovable"`, `compromiseLeaks` naming the vendor-key/side-channel blast radius. `reputational_only` is explicitly wrong here — rivals share no reputation root.

For the replication and image-forensic arms, the output is a `VerificationVerdict` (`state: "verified_partitioned"`): `proven[]` = "reanalysis of the pre-registered endpoint lands in ROR bucket B under TEE replication"; `trusted[]` = TEE-attested facts; `unprovable[]` = "the image was intentionally fabricated", "the technique is causal" (`methodClaimsAreUnprovableAtModelScaleIn2026`). Any image-reuse finding is a `ReuseEvidence` **contestable exhibit** (`pValue`, `falsePositiveBound`, `hopDepthCovered`, state `asserted→contested→upheld/rebutted_independent_discovery`) — never a boolean "fabricated." Patient- or figure-level disclosure requires `Release.ownerDecision` (a human act). The `ReceiptPayload` sets `noPerfectSecrecyClaim: true`, carries `ReceiptCaveat`s (`bounded_leakage`, `not_anonymity`, `not_semantic_proof`), and per party a `CrossChamberReceipt` with asymmetric caveats. Standing non-claims it must carry: method-verification-impossible, doubly-sealed-verifier-saw-both-secrets, no-auxiliary-knowledge, receipts-are-not-contracts.

## 3. Entropy-budget economics

**What leaks per query.** A single bucketed disproportionality answer for (drug, term) in Meridian's chamber leaks: presence (~1 `fieldPresenceBit`: does Meridian hold ≥1 case), the ROR bucket (~2–3 `enumValueBits` over the legal bucket count), and timing. An `absence` emission is 1 bit — but over K MedDRA terms it is K bits, and K terms across the hierarchy reconstructs the whole portfolio. A per-record forensic verdict (this image, this duplicate case) is a near-full-identification event — potentially the entire subject in one emission.

**Adversarial-maximum charging.** `schemaBits = enumValueBits + orderingBits + fieldPresenceBits`, each log2 of its legal choice space, summed and audited, with the estimator role-separated. Honest-case charging is a silent hole; the accountant assumes steganographic intent.

**Composition over repeated annotation.** `CompositionState.cumulativeStructuredBits` is monotone against `structuredBitsCeiling` keyed on (subject, queryFamily, audience=rival, window). The presence channel is `repeatedQueryComposition: "blocked"` (each presence probe is expensive and rate-limited); bucketed ROR is `"bounded"`. This is exactly the ip_trade_sim's over-probing-to-steal-a-technique attack, ported to portfolio enumeration.

**Rough numbers.** An `EgressBudget` for observer = rival-Corvane over the drug class might carry ~8–16 structured bits per audience-window: enough for a handful of bucketed answers, not enough to enumerate presence across the MedDRA tree. Harm is nonlinear in bits (frontier #4): naming one fabricated image is one bit and a $15M FCA exposure plus a career — so the forensic verdict prices consequence separately from bits, never treating an n=1 disclosure as "just one bit."

**Why the buyer pays.** The FDA/HTA buyer's alternative is a label change forced on a fragmented, incomplete class signal (litigation, deaths) or years of consortium negotiation that never touches raw narratives. Against $15M-settlement / label-change stakes, a bounded cross-manufacturer reanalysis is worth six-to-seven figures. The manufacturer-owner is paid a `ReservePrice` exceeding its competitive cost of revealing one bounded bit, and — critically — with `purposeDisclosure: "none_required"`, so admitting the agent does not itself signal "we have a problem." **The economic unlock:** today ~15% IPD availability because holders cannot bound open-ended reanalysis leakage and therefore refuse. A hard per-observer `structuredBitsCeiling` converts "unbounded risk → refuse" into "priced, bounded risk → admit for $X." The budget *is* the product the holder sells.

## 4. The recurring feedback loop and licensing design

This is standing post-market surveillance, not a one-shot audit. A regulator/HTA/journal funds a `CreditPool` and posts a `Bounty` (`targetSchemaId` = disproportionality / pre-registered-replication / image-forensic schema; `acceptanceRule.maxLeakage: "bounded"`, `evaluatorRoleSeparated: true`). The bounty creates **no access** — authority flows only through each owner's `Grant`. The admitted agent runs and emits `Annotation`s. An `EvaluatorOracle` — a *pinned* methods/reproducibility rubric (MedDRA version + statistical protocol + `rubricHash`, `determinism: "sampled_majority"`, `appealPath: "second_oracle"`) — scores them; the oracle's author must clear a `ConflictOfInterestCheck` over `BeneficialEntity` (an oracle upgrade is a *new* oracle). `Acceptance` settles owner-internal; a `SettlementPayoutAuthorization` (bound once by a human, to that oracle + `PriceSchedule` + match predicate, with per-payout and window ceilings) lets `CreditSettlement` pay the reviewer/agent zero-touch — while content disclosure keeps its separate per-`Release` `ownerDecision`. Money and disclosure are deliberately split. `Bounty.resetWindow: "monthly"` plus an `AnnotationFeed`/`FeedSubscription` turns each new AE data increment into a re-run of the disproportionality family, paying per accepted signal-update.

Licensing is consent-first. The pooled cross-manufacturer class signal is jointly-produced IP: each contributing chamber signs a `LicenseGrant` (`assetId` = its bounded contribution, `auditRights: "attestation_only"`) **before** work (`schedulesBindBeforeWorkStarts`). Downstream reuse — a payer, another manufacturer, an FDA submission — is evidenced by a licensee-**signed** `ReuseAttestation` (in-toto/DSSE style). If a manufacturer folds the pooled signal into a submission without attribution, `ReuseEvidence` (`method: "statistical_similarity"`, `pValue`, `falsePositiveBound`) is the contestable exhibit — never a boolean "reused" — with `SlashableBond.consequenceRoot: "jurisdictional_arbitration"` (sovereign rivals will not bond on-chain; where no root exists the type records `"none"` rather than pretend). Cryptographic receipts are explicitly **not** self-enforcing contracts; enforcement is extra-substrate.

## 5. The wedge: first customer, first 90 days, what is billed

**First customer: an HTA body or journal, not the rivals.** The trustless-rival PV case is the sharpest capability but the hardest sale. The wedge is a single-owner arm where one party controls both the data and the motive to admit an agent: an **HTA body (NICE/ICER) funds a bounty for independent reanalysis of one contested oncology trial** on a Vivli-style substrate, or a **journal commissions a pre-registered-endpoint replication plus image-integrity exhibit inside a sponsor's chamber.**

*Days 0–30:* stand up one `Chamber` over one trial's IPD or one lab's figures; pin the `EvaluatorOracle` rubric (reproducibility protocol + MedDRA/analysis version); wire the `EgressBudget`/`CompositionState`/`DenominatorGuard` and the `tee_attested` `EnvRecipe`. *Days 30–60:* run the agent, emit the `VerificationVerdict` partition and the `ReuseEvidence` exhibit; exercise `ownerDecision` on any n=1 disclosure. *Days 60–90:* deliver the signed `CrossChamberReceipt`/`ReceiptPayload` to the commissioner; convert to a `resetWindow: "monthly"` `FeedSubscription` for standing surveillance.

**What is billed:** the commissioner pays into the `CreditPool` per accepted `Annotation` (via `PriceSchedule`), and the data-holder is paid a `ReservePrice` per bounded disclosure with `purposeDisclosure: "none_required"`. The substrate takes a clearing fee on each `PriceCross`. The billable unit is the *bounded, receipted verdict*, not access.

## 6. Missing primitives, with admission-test verdicts (proposed as canon changes)

Four candidates, screened against Boundary/Lifecycle/Composition/Owner/Receipt:

1. **`PresenceReserve` — PASSES ALL FIVE.** The #1 gate. `entropy.ts` has `ObservableKind: "absence"` but no way for an owner to *reserve* presence/absence for a (scope, term) so that a true-zero and a deliberate refusal emit through one padded channel, indistinguishable, debited as a single fixed bit. Refusal-vs-true-absence is otherwise folklore that leaks the competitive secret. Repeated refusals across the MedDRA tree reconstruct the portfolio exactly as presence would, so they must compose against the same ceiling. **Companion change:** add `"refusal"` to `ObservableKind` (today only `"absence"` exists) so the accountant can charge it.

2. **`NonAggregateForensicWarrant` — PASSES ALL FIVE.** The typed n=1 carve-out. Proven/trusted/unprovable forensics names *one* image or *one* duplicate case — precisely the small-cell event `DenominatorGuard` blocks. Today the guard either silently kills legitimate forensics or is silently punctured. This record is granted by a named adjudicating authority, revocable, audited, and debited at full re-identification cost. Receipt: "this verdict names one record; re-identification risk was accepted by authority Z under consequence root R."

3. **`AdjudicatedExhibit` / `PanelCountersignature` — PASSES only as a pointer+quorum wrapper.** An ORI/FDA/EMA panel adopting a `VerificationVerdict`/`ReuseEvidence` with a stated consequence root is the move that converts an exhibit into an actionable finding and gives defamation cover. Its composition leg is weak: a countersignature bounds no leakage of its own, so it must **inherit** composition from the exhibit it adopts. Defined as a standalone data carrier it **FAILS** (no new emission surface it governs); defined as a quorum wrapper binding an existing verdict id, borrowing `SettlementConsortium`'s threshold shape, it **PASSES**.

4. **`CrossChamberRecordLinkage` — PASSES ALL FIVE.** `matching.ts` `CandidateRelation` matches Principals (people/orgs), not AE-case *records* across rival chambers — where confirming "Meridian's case = Corvane's case" itself leaks that Meridian holds that patient event. Lifecycle `proposed/consent_pending/released/expired`; repeated linkage probes reconstruct the patient set, so it debits `CompositionState`; each owner consents via `ConsentPolicy` (denial invisible). Receipt: "probable duplicate, p<X, identity NOT confirmed." A genuine sibling of `CandidateRelation` over records.

**Declined (named, not primitivized):** (a) **`OwnerQuorum`** for institutional chambers — a pharma safety org / IRB / ORI panel is a quorum, but `Chamber.ownerId` is one `Principal` (frontier #8), and a quorum record rests on proof-of-uniqueness (#1). Named; `ownerDecision` stays single-Principal with a caveat. (b) **`PriorRelativeBudget`** — the no-auxiliary-knowledge gap (frontier #5) is acute because rival-Corvane has a strong public prior over the drug class, so absolute-bit budgets over-charge honest queries and under-charge Corvane specifically; but the prior is itself unbounded, so this stays a standing receipt non-claim, not a record.

## 7. Open-frontier items this domain leans on

- **#13 Doubly-sealed verifier.** One agent in both rivals' chambers *is* the trust model. Unsolved: a compromised enclave leaks Meridian's method to Corvane. The receipt names it; the `tee_vendor_root` degrades to `explicit_unprovable`. If the vendor key breaks, the whole PV cooperation collapses — this is the single largest kill vector.
- **#12 Verification-as-extraction.** Probing presence across the MedDRA tree *is* portfolio theft. Handled as a typed `repeated_query_reconstruction` block — but the ceiling is a heuristic tripwire, not a theorem.
- **#5 No auxiliary-knowledge model.** Absolute-bit budgets are the uncredited Dwork/Roth gap; a rival's public prior makes honest budgets simultaneously too tight and too loose. Stays a non-claim.
- **#4 Harm nonlinear in bits.** One forensic bit = one destroyed career + $15M. The bit accountant is decoupled from `Scope.sensitivity`; `NonAggregateForensicWarrant` prices consequence separately, but the coupling is asserted, not derived.
- **#8 Multi-owner authority & #9 purpose enforcement.** `declaredPurpose` is an unenforced string; institutional owners are quorums modeled as single Principals. A regulator could, in principle, launder a fishing expedition as a "class reanalysis."
- **#15 Extra-substrate enforcement.** Sovereign rivals bond nothing; `consequenceRoot: "none"` is the honest common case. The receipt is evidence for an arbitrator, not a contract.

Because these are unsolved, the honest product is "priced, bounded, *contestable*" — never "proven safe."

## 8. Honest kill-risks

1. **The clean-room "just an egress DSL" reframe.** A skeptical CTO says the composition accountant + absence-emission + typed receipts is a policy layer bolted onto decentriq/Azure CC. The rebuttal is narrow but real: that bolt-on *is* the abandonment of the pre-agreed-fixed-computation trust model (DataSHIELD pre-registered functions, OHDSI study packages) that defines the incumbent — and DP's noise-vs-forensic-fidelity tradeoff is a theorem, not a config gap. If a clean-room vendor ships absence-metering and per-observer composition next quarter, the moat is the receipt semantics and the market machinery, which are thinner.
2. **Doubly-sealed-verifier compromise.** A TEE side-channel that leaks one rival's narratives to the other ends cross-manufacturer cooperation permanently. Reputational, not just technical, ruin.
3. **The bureaus commoditize it.** EudraVigilance/FAERS already run duplicate management; CIOMS WG XIV (Dec 2025) blesses AI ICSR de-duplication; EHDS (in force March 2025) + HealthData@EU provide a sanctioned secondary-use rail. If regulators mandate a public utility that does 80% of this, the trustless-rival edge shrinks to a niche.
4. **The n=1 forensic verdict is a defamation magnet.** Even a partitioned `unprovable`-heavy verdict invites litigation; without `AdjudicatedExhibit` countersignature adoption, holders may refuse to let any named verdict cross.
5. **The marketplace/bounty framing is approximable.** Vivli + a funder + attestation logs can mimic the administrative layer. The surviving delta is narrow: per-observer full-emission-surface composition (absence, timing, refusal) plus typed non-aggregate forensic receipts. Sell *that*, not the marketplace.
6. **Purpose is unenforced.** A regulator fishing for a competitor's portfolio under a "safety" pretext is not blocked by the type system — only by the bit ceiling, which a patient adversary amortizes.

Survives narrowly and honestly. The strongest single point remains: no incumbent protects the *absence* signal as a metered first-class emission, and that is the literal gate on rivals sharing raw case narratives with no trust root.
