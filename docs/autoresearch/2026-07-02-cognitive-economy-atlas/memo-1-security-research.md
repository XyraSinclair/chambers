# Third-party security research over sealed vendor artifacts

*Memo-grade treatment. Judge 68. Surviving delta: composition-aware egress metering on the research agent's own typed outputs — verification-as-extraction, priced.*

## 1. The silo bind and who must cooperate

Vulnerability research is the one domain where the confidential-computing threat model runs backwards. A normal enclave protects a workload from a hostile host. Here the host (the vendor) is the party with something to lose, and the *workload* — the researcher's autonomous agent — is the adversary. NVIDIA CC or an AMD SEV-SNP confidential container seals the source *from the operator*, which is necessary but does nothing about the real leak: the agent's sanctioned output. A vulnerability repro, a VEX reachability verdict, a detection rule — each is simultaneously the market commodity the vendor is paying for *and* a channel that carries source structure out of the enclave. Confidential computing performs zero output-content accounting. The incumbent fallback is a human reading the report plus DLP regexes, which cannot scale to machine-speed, semantically-rich findings that compose across repeated builds and across many vendors.

That is the bind. A vendor holds crown-jewel source, build config, secrets, SBOMs, and an unpatched-vuln queue. XBOW-class agents demonstrably find real zero-days *when given white-box source access* ([XBOW hit #1 on HackerOne's US leaderboard in 90 days, in every case given source](https://xbow.com/blog/top-1-how-xbow-did-it)). But white-box access *is* source exfiltration, and the channel that proves a bug exists is the same channel that reconstructs the module. Bug bounties give shallow black-box access; internal scans give no external cognition; a CVD coordinator is a human forwarding PGP email. None of these lets external open-ended cognition touch sealed source under a bounded, accounted, priced output channel.

**Who must cooperate:** the independent researcher / autonomous-pentest agent (the admitted adversary); the vendor's PSIRT (the owner); a coordinator such as CERT/CC for multi-party cases (a *mediator*, never a party); the upstream maintainer of a shared component; and, for the detection edge, an enterprise SOC whose sealed telemetry is the ground truth. They are mutually distrusting: disclosure to one vendor can burn a bug at another, and the affected-set of a shared-component flaw is priceless to an attacker.

## 2. The crossing, end-to-end, in canonical nouns

**Scenario.** `wolfden-tls`, a widely embedded TLS library stewarded by the Fenwick Foundation, has an actively-exploited heap CVE. Roughly twenty CRA-regulated device vendors ship it. Meridian Systems (industrial gateways) wants to know, inside the CRA 24-hour early-warning window, whether the flaw is *reachable* in its own firmware — without letting Cardinal Offensive's agent "Corvid" walk off with the Meridian source tree.

1. **Chamber & Scopes.** Meridian's PSIRT is a `Chamber` (`core.ts`), `defaultRelease = review_required`, `RetentionPolicy.disputeHorizon` spanning the CRA liability window. It defines `Scope`s over the crown jewels — source tree (`DataClass work_product`, `sensitivity: high`, `presentation: opaque_handle`, `canaryHash` set), build graph, SBOM, vuln queue — each a `selectorHash`, never a locator.
2. **AgentPackage & Grant.** Cardinal publishes Corvid as a hashed bundle with `declaredOutputSchemas = [VEX-reachability, vuln-report]`. Meridian issues a `Grant` (`granteeId = worker_agent`, `agentHash`, `allowedScopeIds = [source, build graph]`, `sink = SinkPolicy(durableChannel: typed_annotation, schemaId: VEX, maxCapacityBits)`, `valid`: a 24h `TimeWindow`). **This grant is the admission of external white-box cognition into sealed source — the thing no existing market performs.**
3. **EnvRecipe.** `isolation = tee_attested` (`environment.ts`); the NVIDIA CC enclave seals the source *from the host*. Mounts `read_only` with `pathVirtualization: opaque`; `NetworkPolicy.mode = none`; `ModelAccessPolicy(trainingUseForbidden)`; `SecretPolicy(neverVisibleToAgentAuthor)`; `LogPolicy: owner_private`. The `EnvironmentReceiptPayload` carries `claimClass: tee_quote`, caveated — `RUNTIME_LAWS` forbid a container from claiming privacy.
4. **Transform.** `declaredPurpose = "CRA blast-radius wolfden CVE-XXXX"`; `InputPolicy(allowedGranularity: full_read, canRevealSourceLocators: false)`; `OutputPolicy(schemaId: VEX, freeText: forbidden, allowedFieldClasses: [boolean, enum, bucket, capped_text], exactCountsAllowed: false, sourceListsAllowed: false)`; `ReviewPlan(independence: separate_models)`.
5. **Run.** Walks the gates `submit → static_scan → preflight → owner_execution → worker → release_review → owner_disclosure` (`core.ts` `Gate`).
6. **The load-bearing part — every emission is metered.** Each thing Corvid emits is an `ObservableEvent` (`entropy.ts`) under an `ObservablePolicy`, and each is an `EgressDebit` against an `EgressBudget` (`observer: requester`, `scope: grant`, `compositionKeyId`). The `CapacityEstimate` is charged at the **adversarial maximum**: `enumValueBits` (the VEX verdict space), `orderingBits` = log2(k!) over reported call-path permutations, `fieldPresenceBits`, `textBitsUpperBound` = the minimal repro charged at `maxBytes` (a repro can smuggle source, so the honest "it's only three lines" is *not* chargeable), and `sideChannelBits`. The `EstimatorAttestation.independence` must be `role_separated`/`adversarial_review` with `worstCaseOverSecrets: true` — **never `self_interested`**, because the paid agent understating its own side-channel bits would break every downstream ceiling silently.
7. **Composition.** A `CompositionKey` (`subjectHash = wolfden-in-Meridian`, `queryFamilyHash = reachability`, `audienceHash = Cardinal`) accumulates `cumulativeStructuredBits` across repeated queries. `releaseGate` is the *conjunction* of a numeric accountant (`< structuredBitsCeiling`) and an ordinal `EntropyReview`. This is the rib that matters: per-build reachability probes each look safe, but N builds × M sites reconstruct the control-flow graph.
8. **The commodity.** The typed VEX verdict — `{reachable, not-present, needs-human}` plus a bucketed CVSS and a capped minimal repro — is an `Annotation` (`role: risk_flag`, alias `CognitiveDelta`) targeting the source `Scope`, `state: submitted`. The market buys *this*, never access. The minimal-repro `Artifact` is `visibility: owner_private`, `integrity: endorsed_agent`; it is the sharpest tension (strongest evidence, most source-revealing) and cannot leave without `release_review`.
9. **Release & receipt.** Filing to the ENISA platform requires a human `ownerDecision` (`contentDisclosureRequiresAHumanOwnerDecision`); `releasedFields` are a reviewed subset of the sink, with a `DeclassificationWitness` on a high-integrity selector. The `ReceiptPayload` carries **no boolean `verified`**: the `VerificationVerdict` (`iptrade.ts`) partitions into `proven[]` (call path exists under `tee_replication` of the build), `trusted[]` (analyzer soundness), and `unprovable[]` ("only reachable path", "technique generalizes"). `noPerfectSecrecyClaim`; caveat `not_semantic_proof`.

**Cross-vendor blast-radius** rides `negotiation.ts`: a `NegotiationLane` over the ~20 adopter Chambers with CERT/CC as `mediatorId` (never a party). "Is Vendor B also affected?" clears as a `PriceCross` (`pricing.ts`, `counterpartyLearns: one_bit`); B's silence is metered as an `ObservableEvent kind: absence` so it cannot leak "we're hit"; a `no_cross` still debits composition. `EscrowedClaim` commits affectedness by hash; `walkAwayTimingIsItselfAnEmission` models the embargo.

## 3. Entropy-budget economics

**What leaks per annotation.** A clean VEX verdict with no repro is small: `enumValueBits` over three states is under 2 bits, plus the researcher-chosen component (low marginal). The dangerous emission is the minimal repro — `textBitsUpperBound = maxBytes` at adversarial max, plus `orderingBits = log2(k!)` over k reported call paths, plus `fieldPresenceBits`. One accepted VEX-with-repro is bounded by the sink's `maxCapacityBits` and can be hundreds to low-thousands of bits worst-case; one bare reachability verdict is a handful.

**Composition is the real threat.** Each reachability query is ~1 bit ("reachable in build N at site M?"), but `CompositionState` sums them, and N builds × M sites reconstruct the reachability matrix — a large fraction of the control-flow IP. `cumulativeStructuredBits` binds *only* structured channels (enums, orderings, buckets, price crosses) per `numericAccountantBindsStructuredChannelsNotProse`; prose stays on `cumulativeLeakage` + `EntropyReview`. A budget scoped `grant`/`query_family` caps "up to B structured bits of structural observation about component C to researcher R over the CRA window" — enough to locate and prove *one* bug class, not to reconstruct the module. It is a tripwire, not a certificate (`budgetsAreTripwiresNotCertificates`): exhaustion forces `owner_review`; staying under proves nothing.

**Money and bits are decoupled ribs.** The entropy budget is the *constraint* (what the vendor will leak); the bounty price is the *incentive* (what the finding is worth). A CRA-regulated PSIRT faces [24h early-warning / 72h notification obligations from 11 September 2026, fines to €15M or 2.5% of turnover](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) on a clock human triage + DLP cannot meet. So one accepted, non-regressing finding on an actively-exploited CVE prices in the thousands to tens-of-thousands of credits (≈ fine-risk-avoided × P(dispositive)), dwarfing its marginal entropy cost. For the cross-vendor one-bit `PriceCross` the money is small but composition cost is high: `DenominatorGuard` + absence-metering + owner `ReservePrice` make each cross expensive in *composition* budget even at trivial money, because the affected-set is what an attacker would pay most for.

## 4. Feedback loop and licensing/royalty design

**The recurring engine.** A PSIRT, the Fenwick Foundation, or an MDR posts a `Bounty` pinned to an `EvaluatorOracle` (a severity/reproducibility `rubricHash` + `modelClassHash`; `anOracleUpgradeIsANewOracle`) and a `PriceSchedule` (`pricing.ts`: score → credits, `holdbackFraction`, `regressionWindow`). A human binds a `SettlementPayoutAuthorization` *once*, ex-ante, to that oracle + schedule + `matchPredicate` with per-payout and window ceilings.

**Per cycle:** `Grant` admits the agent → agent emits `Annotation` → oracle scores against the pinned rubric → `Acceptance` on an owner-internal decision, with a `ConflictOfInterestCheck` confirming Cardinal's `BeneficialEntity` is disjoint from the oracle author (oracle capture is the failure mode) → `CreditSettlement` lands `status: heldback` → the `regressionWindow` elapses without the shipped fix regressing → payout **released zero-touch** under the standing authorization. Money moves; content keeps its per-`Release` `ownerDecision` gate (`standingAuthorizationsMovePayoutsNeverContent`). Clawback slashes a "fix" that regresses. Each new CVE / build / telemetry window reopens the `Bounty` (`resetWindow`).

**Two objects, licensed differently.** (1) VEX findings and repros are *not* licensed IP — they are accepted `Annotation`s bought by `Bounty` and settled via `CreditSettlement`; the vendor owns the finding about its own product. (2) The reusable discovery *technique* (the backport-aware reachability method) is Cardinal's licensable asset. A `LicenseGrant` binds *before* the technique runs (`royaltyIsConsentFirstNotSurveillance`). If the technique is a `pure_recipe`, `pureRecipeReusePinsToUnprovable` — the substrate declines to sell a royalty it cannot back. `ReuseAttestation` is the consent-first spine: a licensee-signed in-toto/DSSE provenance record no third party had to observe. Only on dispute does `ReuseEvidence` appear, contestable, carrying `pValue`, `falsePositiveBound`, and a `rebutted_independent_discovery` state — watermarks are not robust to a distilling adversary. `SlashableBond` states its `consequenceRoot` honestly (`jurisdictional_arbitration` or `none` for sovereigns who will not bond collateral). Method claims land in `VerificationVerdict.unprovable[]`, never sold as proven.

## 5. The wedge

**First customer: one enterprise, not the cross-vendor consortium.** The cleanest, least-oversold wedge is a *single* CRA-regulated firmware vendor (Meridian) running an XBOW-style agent inside its own attested source enclave, paid per accepted VEX/finding. This needs no coordinator, no multi-party clock, and no joint computation — the parts the gauntlet flagged as decorative.

**First 90 days.** (1) Stand up Meridian's `Chamber` with `Scope`s over one product line and one shared component. (2) Admit Corvid under a 24h `Grant` into a `tee_attested` `EnvRecipe`, `NetworkPolicy.mode: none`. (3) Wire the `EgressBudget` + `CompositionKey` on the VEX `query_family`, with an `EstimatorAttestation` from a role-separated estimator. (4) On the next actively-exploited `wolfden` CVE, produce a filed-quality VEX reachability verdict + minimal repro inside the window, human-gated to release. (5) Pin the `EvaluatorOracle` and bind one `SettlementPayoutAuthorization`.

**What is billed:** per accepted, non-regressing `Annotation` (severity- and reproducibility-scored against the pinned oracle), settled from a `CreditPool` after the `regressionWindow`. The vendor pays for *accounted external cognition on sealed source* — the entropy metering is what makes that a sellable act rather than an act of trust. Secondary billable: an MDR detection-bounty board whose Sigma rules are validated against a design-partner bank's *sealed* telemetry, paid **only on non-regressing measured precision** — structurally beyond SOC Prime, which [pays on unique-company download/deploy traction, not measured precision against sealed telemetry](https://socprime.com/blog/threat-bounty-program-first-steps-to-monetizing-your-detection-engineering-skills/).

## 6. Missing primitives (admission-test verdicts)

Proposed explicitly as canon changes, never asserted as already-canon.

- **`SealedEvalGrant` — STRONGEST, clearly missing. Admit as a new record.** The detection edge scores a submitted rule against a design-partner's *sealed* telemetry that neither the MDR sponsor nor the rule author may see; only bucketed TP/FP rates return. `EvaluatorOracle` assumes the ground truth is the sponsor's own data — here it is a *third* sovereign Chamber's, and the precision verdict returned to the worker is a composing egress channel *from that third party*. **Boundary:** "scored against telemetry owned by neither party" is folklore today. **Lifecycle:** pinned, run-per-submission, retired. **Composition:** passes hard — repeated probe-rules reconstruct the bank's topology and past-breach signatures from returned TP/FP deltas (verification-as-extraction on the *defender*); needs its own `EgressBudget` + `CompositionKey` keyed on the telemetry owner. **Owner:** the telemetry owner (not the sponsor) governs disclosure. **Receipt:** "scored against sealed telemetry; bounded bits leaked to the author via precision deltas; not a claim the telemetry is unreconstructed." Passes all five.
- **`ArtifactLineageCompositionKey` — MOSTLY PASSES. Admit as a minimal extension to `CompositionKey`, not a new module.** `CompositionState` composes over a static `subjectHash`; this domain's threat is reachability queries across successive *builds* of an evolving codebase, where the leaked quantity per crossing is the build *diff*. Add a lineage-aware `subjectHash` field so cumulative structural leak is tracked across a release train. Weak leg: it is a field, not a record — hence "extension," honestly.
- **`WeaponizationHazard` — PASSES only as a frontier-naming record.** A working repro for an actively-exploited RCE is catastrophic at ~zero incremental bits: this is CANON open-frontier #4 (harm not linear in bits). Candidate: a `HazardVerdict` on the repro `Artifact` (`dual_use_regardless_of_bits | embargo_gated | benign`) gating `Release` on an axis *orthogonal* to the bit accountant, expiring on patch-confirmed. Its receipt must record "unprovable-whether-nonlinear-harm-bounded," never a boolean safe — it *names* the frontier, it does not solve it.

Explicitly **not** new primitives: worker-side fair exchange (mitigated by `paymentSettlesOnOwnerInternalAcceptance` + `EscrowedClaim`, but stays open-frontier #2); embargo across N vendors (already carried by `NegotiationLane` + `walkAwayTimingIsItselfAnEmission`; Spectre-style premature reveal is an extra-substrate enforcement gap, #15); source-derivation check on the repro (an `EntropyReview` method + `EstimatorAttestation`, i.e. a payload, not a durable record).

## 7. Open-frontier items this domain leans on

- **#12 Verification-as-extraction** — the domain's central friction *is* this frontier item. The substrate does not *solve* it; it types, meters, and prices it. If the bit-accounting under-charges (e.g. a `self_interested` estimator slips through), the repro channel silently reconstructs source and the vendor is worse off than with human review.
- **#4 Harm nonlinear in bits** — a one-bit "reachable" plus a tiny repro can be a live weapon. Unaddressed by the accountant; `WeaponizationHazard` only names it. Consequence if unsolved: a low-bit release passes the budget yet ships an exploit.
- **#6 Trusted TCB** — the enclave, operator, and (cross-vendor) doubly-sealed verifier are trusted, ledgered post-hoc, not MPC. The doubly-sealed verifier sees both secrets. Consequence: a compromised TEE root or colluding verifier leaks A's source to B; receipts must degrade loudly (`TrustRoot.degradesTo`).
- **#11 Method verification** — "this technique is novel / transfers" is unprovable at model scale; royalty compounding rests on `unprovable[]` claims and consent-first attestation, not proof.
- **#8 Multi-owner authority** — `Chamber.ownerId` is a single Principal, but a PSIRT is a team with quorum. Noted, not asserted away; the wedge deliberately picks a single-owner framing to avoid leaning on this.
- **#15 Extra-substrate enforcement** — cryptographic receipts are not contracts; embargo-breaking, export control, and trade-secret forfeiture live in jurisdiction, not types.

## 8. Honest kill-risks

1. **The CRA cross-vendor clock is oversold.** The [ENISA Single Reporting Platform launches 11 Sept 2026 as a web portal with no API](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security-and-certification/single-reporting-platform-srp), and the obligation is **per-manufacturer for its own product** — not a mandated joint cross-vendor computation. Each vendor already knows its own product ships the component. The "synchronized 24h cross-vendor blast-radius clock" is not a real regulatory forcing function. The wedge must stand on *single-vendor* accounted cognition, which it does.
2. **The coordinator does not need removing.** CERT/CC (VINCE) is a legally-mandated, funded, trusted TTP the buyers already use. "Cross-vendor blast-radius without a trusted coordinator" is a solution to a problem nobody is paying to have solved. The domain survives on the *output-metering* delta, not on disintermediating the coordinator.
3. **The survivor is a mechanism-and-pricing advantage, not a completed proof.** Verification-as-extraction, the doubly-sealed verifier, trusted TCB, and method-verification are all *open frontier*. What survives is: this is the only stack that types, meters, prices, and honestly non-claims the exfil channel that *is* the domain's friction — strictly more than "a human eyeballs the report," which cannot scale to machine-speed composing findings. If a vendor decides human review + DLP is *good enough* for its finding volume, the wedge has no pull.
4. **Estimator soundness is load-bearing and hard.** The whole guarantee collapses if `textBitsUpperBound` or `orderingBits` is under-charged, or if the adversarial estimator is not truly role-separated. Adversarial-max charging is also *conservative to the point of friction* — it may reject honest findings, souring researchers.
5. **`SealedEvalGrant` is unbuilt.** The SOC-Prime-beating detection edge depends on a primitive that does not yet exist in canon; until it does, that edge is a design claim, not a shippable feature.
6. **Vendors may simply refuse to admit external agents into crown-jewel source at all** — cultural/legal risk-aversion that no type system overcomes. The market may not clear on the source side regardless of how well the output channel is metered.
